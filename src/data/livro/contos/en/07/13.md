# CHAPTER 13 — The Fourth Chain

**JACK**

Up to then, nothing wrong. At least nothing off the plan.

I was causing chaos. A gun here and there went off, obviously, but it was too confusing for the guys to aim at anyone. Kim was tearing it up.

And I kept making sure. Dropping one here, another there, to end it fast. Before Kim crossed the line.

That's when I heard him yelling.

— You know the only rule of a street fight? You know the only rule of a street fight?!

---

When I looked, there were three guys aiming at his back.

Up to that moment, Kim didn't stay on any one guy for any time at all. But right there he got distracted.

And I had nothing left to throw. No bottle, no pipe, nothing.

So I took off a sneaker and threw a spinning kick.

The sneaker flew. And I got insanely lucky: it hit the first one's hand, his hand hit the second one's hand, the second one lost his balance onto the third.

All three shots went wide.

---

I needed to get to Kim. I threw the other sneaker at him, so he'd snap out of it.

Didn't work. His instinct was so sharp he jumped off the guy he was on and dodged the sneaker without even looking.

And went for the next one.

I was left in my socks in the middle of a shootout.

**KIM**

I don't remember the sneaker.

I have some flashes. They're distant. Very distant.

I have a flash of my hands red. Bathed in blood. Really dirty.

I start wiping them. Wipe them on my face. Wipe them on my clothes.

I have a flash of my clothes torn. Completely torn. I don't know if they tore them or if I did.

And the rest are blurs. I jumped from one side to the other, fast. Every blur, a guy on the floor. Screaming or out cold. Always with something broken.

---

**JACK**

It was a horror scene.

Fire. Screams. Gunshots. And every time someone screamed, it was because Kim had gone after someone.

When I finally got a good look at Kim, his face was covered in blood. With the finger marks from where he'd tried to wipe it off.

That's when I realized.

Four chains.

---

You need to understand something.

With three chains loose, Kim doesn't care about the line anymore.

With four, he *wants* to cross it. He stops thinking about anything else. He's still Kim. But he becomes something else too.

The only reason he's never done something truly fucked up is because I was there every single time.

And, being honest, I don't know if I can handle Kim with four chains loose.

---

There were still dudes standing. And the fire was starting to spread.

We needed to get Helena out of there. Fast.

And I heard it again.

— You know the only rule?

Kim was hanging off a big fat guy, holding him by the neck. Squeezing slowly.

The fat guy was punching him, one after another. Kim wouldn't let go. He just smiled and squeezed.

The fat guy started running out of air. One leg wobbled. Then the other.

---

I ran to Kim like a madman.

With the force he was putting in, he was going to crush the guy's windpipe. And then there'd be no coming back.

The fat guy was already on the floor, with Kim on top. I stomped on Kim's arm, right in the crook of the elbow, to break the force. His arms bent. He let go.

Then he turned to me.

And it wasn't the Kim I know anymore.

**KIM**

We own what we do. It was me.

**JACK**

It was. But it was a side of you I'd never seen.

---

He came at me full force.

I tried to dodge. For real, I took some hits. Several.

There were only about three guys left standing. They watched without getting it. Until they got it.

And took advantage.

They came from behind and started hammering me. With pieces of iron, the kind they must've found in the room.

One of them hit my head.

I felt the hot blood running down my neck, down my back. And I started fading. Slowly.

And, of course, Kim wasn't going to let me off cheap. A punch to my chin, to speed up the fall.

From there on I don't know a thing.
