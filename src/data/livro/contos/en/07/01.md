# CHAPTER 1 — I Don't Tell Stories

I don't like telling stories.

I'm not good at it either. Nobody needs to point that out. I know that when I tell a story, it comes out like shit.

**JACK**

It really does.

**KIM**

Thanks, Jack.

Jack has a way of telling things that's all his. He takes any random thing and makes it interesting, funny, the kind of thing you remember later. I don't.

**JACK**

I'm very good at it, by the way.

**KIM**

I think it's ridiculous. Not the people who do it. Some people are good at it. I think *me* doing it is ridiculous.

But I'm going to tell it anyway. So you understand what Jack means by *chains*.

And since I don't remember half of it, he's going to tell it with me. When it's him talking, you'll know.

**JACK**

You'll know because it gets better.

---

**KIM**

Chains. Jack's the one who came up with the name.

I don't name things. Jack names everything. Everything that gets near him gets its own exclusive name, and from then on it's his. He takes things for himself. Even mine.

**JACK**

If I don't name it, nobody will. Somebody's gotta organize this mess.

**KIM**

I'm not complaining. It's Jack.

---

Jack is special to me.

He saw me in a way nobody else ever did. He saw my best. He saw my worst. He saw me from the inside, all of me, nothing left to hide.

And he didn't leave.

That's rare. Rarer than people think.

**JACK**

…

**KIM**

For the first time in his life he's got nothing to say.

**JACK**

I'm emotional, Kim. Let me be.

---

**KIM**

Jack is the kind of guy nobody can hate. Even our enemies end up becoming his friends. I don't know how he does it. He doesn't know either. It just happens.

I don't have that. I push people away.

I'm not saying it so anyone feels sorry for me. It's just a fact. People don't want to be around me, and I don't care much for them either.

I like Jack. That's it.

**JACK**

I know.

**KIM**

One is enough. Better one real friend, one I can call for anything, anytime, than a hundred fake ones at my side.

---

I was going to start with the day I got home and…

No. There's something else first.

See? I told you I was bad at this.
