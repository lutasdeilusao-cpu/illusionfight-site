# CHAPTER 9 — The Boost

**JACK**

When I got upstairs, I saw the damage.

Nobody was left standing in the bar. Blood was painting the floor. You could see bone sticking out on more than one guy.

But it wasn't the massacre that worried me.

It was Kim.

---

Kim was different.

I'd seen him serious before. But this was something else. Serious serious.

He'd let loose a few chains.

**KIM**

He calls it that. I don't call it anything.

**JACK**

I call it that because that's what it is. Normal Kim thinks about consequences. That one didn't give a damn. The few still awake on the floor had limbs sticking out. He had no mercy on anybody.

I was gonna say something. Didn't get the chance.

Kim just looked at me and said:

— Jack.

That's it. And I jumped behind the bar counter without even thinking.

---

Kim threw himself behind an iron table.

And the hail of gunfire started from upstairs, all over the place.

There wasn't much to do. I was behind a bar. Not that I wasn't taking it seriously. But there was booze back there.

I picked a bottle up off the floor and took a swig. To really wake up.

The good stuff went down burning.

I couldn't lift my head. I didn't know how Kim was doing.

**KIM**

I stayed behind the table a few seconds. Realized it wasn't going to take that many bullets. I got out and found better cover.

And I waited.

A crew like that usually has one or two kinds of gun, tops. See the first one, everybody's got the same, except for one or two. Fifteen-, twenty-round mags.

With that many shots at once, it's hard to count. But you don't need to. These people aren't trained. They shoot till the mag runs out.

When the click-click starts, it's our turn.

---

**JACK**

I heard the click. Popped up from the bar and yelled:

— Kim!

And I started throwing bottles to him.

Kim caught every one in the air and hurled it at the first floor. Pow. Pow. The bottles burst on the wall, and the glass flew onto the guys who were reloading.

You could hear the screams. You could hear guns hitting the floor.

Then Kim started throwing bottles at the stairs. One after another.

I was taking it seriously, I said that already. But my little bottle was right there. I'd take a nip between one bottle and the next.

**KIM**

He took about five nips.

**JACK**

Three. Four, tops.

---

**KIM**

— Give me a boost.

**JACK**

I jumped the counter right away. Laced my hands together at knee height.

Kim came running. Stepped in my hands. And I launched him up with everything I had.

**KIM**

With the boost, I reached the edge of the first floor.

Right off, there was a guy trying to reload, his hand full of glass shards.

I grabbed him and threw him over.

**JACK**

All I saw was a shape falling in front of me. Didn't know what it was.

I brought my knee to my chest and fired the sole of my foot, straight, into the middle of him. One kick, clean and direct.

The guy flew forward. Out cold.

---

Kim stayed up there. I could only hear.

Screams. Punches. Knives rolling. Stuff falling. Broken glass coming down the stairs.

And then some guys started showing up coming down.

No shoes. Definitely not from the club. Must've been customers, caught in the middle of the party.

That's when I got why Kim had stuffed the stairs with glass. A trap. Everyone who ran down stepped barefoot on the shards.

**KIM**

I didn't plan it. It just seemed right.

**JACK**

That's called planning, Kim.

---

I looked and thought: alright, my turn.

I grabbed the chain and started swinging it. The first one who showed up, I wrapped it around his leg and yanked.

Made the guy come down the whole staircase on his back, step by step, the glass cutting up his back.

When he got to the bottom, my foot went straight to his chin.

It hurt. It hurt a lot. His tongue was sticking out, and his teeth shut on it.

---

Then I started going up.

Tonfa in hand, legs only, dropping every one who came down into Kim's own trap.

The stairs were sticky. Booze, blood and glass.

And me going up, step by step, to back Kim up on the first floor.
