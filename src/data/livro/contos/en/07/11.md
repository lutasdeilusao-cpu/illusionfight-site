# CHAPTER 11 — The Line

**JACK**

When Kim and I fight, there's a line.

We always respect that line. Try to stay as far away from it as we can.

Problem is, when Kim lets loose a chain, he gets a little closer to the line. Every chain, closer.

From the third one on, he doesn't see the line anymore.

Actually, it's worse. He sees it. He just doesn't care anymore. He crosses the line every time, for the pure pleasure of crossing it.

And that's a problem.

---

The guy was on the floor, his hand with the fingers bent backward, crying and screaming.

Kim turned his back on him.

And, back turned and all, he stomped the guy's face against the wall with his heel. Three times. To make sure he wasn't waking up.

— Bro — I said. — Take it easy, goddamn it.

He looked at me. He had a slight smile on his face. The smile of someone enjoying himself.

— No promises.

---

That's when I got really worried.

I'd seen Kim let loose a few chains before. But three was new. Real new.

Kim and I have almost telepathic communication. We don't need to say anything. One looks, the other already knows.

Only in that mode he keeps drifting away. I keep losing his signal.

**KIM**

I don't remember that.

**JACK**

I know. That's why I'm telling it.

---

Out of nowhere, Kim ran.

It's rare for me not to know what Kim's going to do. This time I didn't see it coming. I went after him, obviously, but he was already ahead.

He went up to the second floor. Found the fake wall the guy had given up. Looked like a wall. It was a door to somewhere else.

And he started slamming himself into it. Once. Again. Again. Like a madman. Like an animal in a cage.

*Bam.*

The fake wall cracked and swung open.

---

Behind it was a huge room.

Stacks of cash. Drugs. Some guns on the table.

And people. A whole crowd.

The guys looked at the door, stunned.

Kim came through the door on all fours.

On all fours, on the floor, like a panther that just walked into the room. And his smile growing.

---

— It's not the cops — one of them said. — We thought it was the cops.

They were getting ready to run. Suitcase open, money inside.

It wasn't the cops, no.

It was worse.
