# CHAPTER 10 — Illusion Fight

Kim picked up the guitar again and started tuning.

He turned a peg, plucked a string, listened, turned it again. No tuner, no app. By ear.

Nina walked around the garage with her cup in her hand. In the corner, behind the cardboard boxes, leaning against the wall, there was another instrument. Longer than the guitar. Four thick strings.

— Is this a bass?

— Yep. — Jack, without taking his eyes off the drum box he was tightening by hand. — I fixed that one up because I figured the second thing I was gonna learn in life was bass. 'Cause this band is always missing a bassist. — He pointed at Kim with a drumstick. — This guy's been saying that since we were twelve.

— I never said that.

— You say it with your eyes.

Nina ran her hand over the bass strings. A thin layer of dust rose.

— You guys mind if I grab it?

Jack and Kim looked at each other.

— Go nuts. — Jack.

She picked up the bass, sat on the chair with the patched leg, and started tuning. Kim heard the first string. Heard the second. Stopped messing with the guitar for a second.

She was tuning by ear too.

---

— Done. — Kim, sliding the strap over his shoulder. — It's in tune. Which one you wanna do, man?

Jack spun both drumsticks at the same time.

— Let's open with *Illusion Fight*.

Kim stared at him.

— Dude. Seriously. No way. — He pointed his pick at Jack. — You're a son of a bitch. After everything I went through today. After what happened in that arena. You want to open with *Illusion Fight*.

— It's our best one.

— It's your favorite one.

— Which makes it our best one. Let's go.

[Illusion Fight](/radio-nina/lutas-de-ilusao)

---

— Do you guys have the chords for this? — Nina, settling the bass on her lap. — The lyrics?

Kim and Jack looked at each other again.

Nobody had ever asked for the chords. Nobody had ever been there to ask.

— I'm writing them down. — Jack, already pulling his phone out of his pocket. — Hang on. I have everything in a file. I'll send you the link.

Nina's phone buzzed. She opened it, scrolled down, went back, scrolled again. Her fingers moved to the bass neck and started finding the notes without making a sound, on top of the strings, like someone reading something under their breath.

One minute. Two.

Jack drummed his fingers on the edge of the box.

— So, people? We doing this? Is it starting or not? Pretty soon I'm gonna be drunk.

---

Kim started slow.

A guitar riff. Dry, short, heavy, repeating. The pick scraping the low string, the left hand muting at just the right time.

And he was shy.

It was the first time. He realized it halfway through the first bar. In all those years, in that garage, it had only ever been him and Jack. Nobody watching. Nobody listening, except Helena yelling from the living room to cut the noise.

Now there was someone.

He looked at Nina.

She was focused. Eyes on the bass neck, her mouth moving slightly, counting. She seemed to be taking it more seriously than he was. More seriously than Jack. He didn't understand why.

The riff looped. Looped again. And again. Three times more than it needed to.

Jack slammed the snare so hard the light bulb swung on its wire.

— GO ALREADY, DAMMIT! START ALREADY!

Kim closed his eyes.

And sang.

```letra
KIM: There's only one rule in a street fight,
KIM: There's only one rule in a street fight,
> On the third time, Jack comes in with him.
KIM AND JACK: There's only one rule in a street fight...
```

Jack's drumsticks clicked against each other. One, two, three, four.

And the drums came in.

The torn snare cracked. The kick drum hit everyone in the chest. The dented cymbal hissed. The hole where a tom should have been made Jack skip that beat and land on the next one, and somehow that became the way the song went.

```letra
JACK: Finding new weapons, we've got to get ready,
JACK: Into the unknown, we're throwing ourselves steady.

KIM: I've thought, I've tried, I've changed, I've come back around,
KIM: Thinking of the infinite,
KIM: Finally found the enemy,

KIM AND JACK: GOOOOOO, ohhhh...
KIM AND JACK: And in the middle of the road...
```

And then came something neither of them expected.

---

The bass.

Low, fat, coming in under Kim's guitar and Jack's drums like ground appearing beneath someone who's falling. Both of them felt it in their bodies before they understood it.

And the voice.

Nina opened her mouth and the garage changed size.

```letra
NINA: Tears fall when the night comes down,
NINA: The screams of despair rise above the sound,
NINA: Hidden wishes, dreams pushed down,
NINA: They stare me straight in the face...
```

Kim opened his eyes and looked at her.

Jack looked at her.

Both of them stunned. Both of them still playing. The song wouldn't let them stop.

It was Jack who grabbed the wheel. He always did. He started directing the song while playing, while singing, yelling over the noise, pointing his drumstick at whoever needed to come in.

```letra
> Jack yells: — Kim, your turn!
KIM: Face it!

JACK: But if I fall, I'm gonna get back up,
> — Kim, go!
KIM: If I'm with you, I can change it all,
JACK: I'll never let your fear lead me on,
> Kim comes in without Jack telling him.
KIM: If I've taken the first step, I'll keep going strong.

> — Nina, your turn!
NINA: With me it will always be this way,
> — Everybody!
ALL: I'm gonna run
> Just Kim. Jack yells, and he goes.
KIM: and fight until the end!
```

Jack sucked in air and let out a voice that nobody knew where it came from. Torn, guttural, from the bottom of his stomach.

```letra
JACK: Time to fight, time to fight, time to fight!
> — Everybody, quick stop! Go, Kim!
KIM: Illusion Fight
> — Everybody back in!
ALL: guided my heart,
> Nina takes over.
NINA: Gave me direction
> — Your turn, Kim, damn it! Put some feeling into it, fuck! Nina, trade off with me on the power!
KIM: In search of power,
JACK: power,
NINA: power!
```

---

Something was happening in that garage.

Jack could feel it. He was hitting harder than he hit. Kim was playing faster than he knew how to play. Nina was laughing in the middle of a bass line, without missing a single note.

The three of them looked at each other and nobody needed to say anything. Each one knew where the other was going to come in a second before the other came in. It was that thing Kim and Jack had in a fight. Except now there were three.

Nina pulled the chorus.

```letra
NINA: Illusion Fight in another dimension,
NINA: Sets my being free...
> Kim comes in alone.
KIM: What I want is to win, win, win!
```

And Nina saw.

Kim was smiling.

Eyes closed, head low over the guitar, pick flying, and smiling. Not the half-smile of someone about to hit someone. A full smile, open, like a kid's. A smile she was sure nobody at that school had ever seen.

She looked at Jack.

Jack was already looking at her. He winked.

*That's it.* She understood. *That's the thing he does and doesn't realize.*

---

Jack raised a drumstick.

— This one's mine. Nobody gets in the way.

And the rap came.

```letra
JACK: Broken, thought through, destroyed, carried along,
JACK: My true power is believing
JACK: That I can face anything.
JACK: And if you're standing by my side,
JACK: Nobody can ever stop us! Ah, ah, ah,
JACK: So hold my hand,
JACK: We'll make a mess
JACK: That will be remembered,
JACK: Lived and told
JACK: Until the end of time, eternalized...
JACK: Our names will always be spoken!

JACK: It's time, yeah...
```

— KIM, RIP THE SOLO!

Kim ripped it.

Fast. Dirty. The pick going down and up at a speed the old guitar could barely handle, the mismatched tuning peg groaning, one note tearing into the next.

— DESTROY IT! DESTROY IT! — Jack. — NINA, JUST KEEP THE POCKET! LET'S GO FASTER!

Faster.

More.

And they stopped.

All together, on the same beat, with nobody counting. The silence fell on the garage with the weight of a cymbal someone grabbed with their hand.

One second.

---

Kim came back calm. Almost whispering. And Nina answered from the other side.

```letra
KIM: But if I fall, I'm gonna get back up,
NINA: If I'm with you, I can change it all,
KIM: I'll never let your fear lead me on,
NINA: If I've taken the first step, I'll keep going strong.
NINA: With me it will always be this way,
KIM: I'm gonna run and fight until the end!
```

— Drums and voices only! — Jack. — Come on, people!

```letra
> Guitar and bass drop out. Just the kick drum, the torn snare, and three voices.
ALL: Illusion Fight guided my heart,
ALL: Gave me direction,
ALL: In search of power, power, power!
```

— EVERYBODY BACK IN! HEAVY ENDING! LET'S CLOSE THIS SHIT WITH A GOLDEN KEY!

```letra
ALL: Illusion Fight in another dimension,
ALL: Sets my being free...
ALL: What I want is to win, win, win!

ALL: Illusion Fight guided my heart,
ALL: Gave me direction,
ALL: In search of power, power, power!

ALL: Illusion Fight in another dimension,
ALL: Sets my being free...
ALL: What I want is to win, win, win!

ALL: Illusion Fight guided my heart,
ALL: Gave me direction,
ALL: In search of power, power, power!

ALL: Illusion Fight in another dimension,
ALL: Sets my being free...
ALL: What I want is to win, win, win!
```

— Done.

Jack grabbed the cymbals with his hand.

And only Kim's guitar was left. The same riff from the beginning. Dry. Short. Heavy.

```letra
KIM: There's only one rule in a street fight,
KIM: There's only one rule in a street fight,
KIM AND JACK: There's only one rule in a street fight...
```

---

The last chord hung in the air until it died on its own.

Nobody said anything.

The light bulb was still swinging on its wire. Sweat was running down Jack's forehead. Nina's fingers were red from pulling on the thick strings. Kim was standing still with his open hand on the strings, muting, staring at the floor.

The three of them looked at each other.

— Man. — Jack, out of breath. — That was so fucking awesome.

— That was incredible. — Nina. She was laughing, and she didn't know why. — Jack. How did you manage to coordinate a song like that? How did you know who was going to come in where?

Jack spun a drumstick between his fingers.

— I didn't coordinate anything, man. — He said it seriously, in a way that wasn't very Jack. — The song was there. It has its own power. I was just showing you what it wanted us to do.

He looked at Kim. Looked at Nina.

— And it was sick with the three of us. With the three of us it became a different song.

Kim didn't say anything.

He hung the guitar back on the nail. Slowly. The way you put away something that matters.
