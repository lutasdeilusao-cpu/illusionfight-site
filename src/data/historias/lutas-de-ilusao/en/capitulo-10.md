# CHAPTER 10 — Illusion Fight

Kim picked up the guitar again and started tuning.

He turned a tuning peg, plucked a string, listened, turned it again. No tuner, no app. By ear.

Nina walked through the garage. In the corner, behind the cardboard boxes, leaning against the wall, there was another instrument. Longer than the guitar. Four thick strings.

— This is a bass?

— Yeah. — Jack, without taking his eyes off the drum box, which he was tightening with his hand. — I fixed that one up because I figured the second thing I was going to learn in life was bass. Because this band is always missing a bass player. — He pointed at Kim with the drumstick. — That guy's been saying that since he was twelve.

— I never said that.

— He says it with his eyes.

Nina ran her hand over the bass strings. Fine dust rose.

— Do you guys mind if I take it?

Jack and Kim looked at each other.

— Go ahead, mess with it all you want. — Jack.

She picked up the bass, sat in the chair with the repaired leg, and started tuning. Kim heard the first string. Heard the second. Stopped messing with the guitar for a second.

She was tuning by ear too.

---

— Ready. — Kim, fitting the strap over his shoulder. — It's tuned. Which one do you want to play, man?

Jack spun both drumsticks at the same time.

— Let's open with *Illusion Fight*.

Kim looked at him.

— Dude. Seriously. No way. — He pointed the pick at Jack. — You're a son of a bitch. After everything I went through today. After what happened in that arena. You want to open with *Illusion Fight*.

— It's our best.

— It's your favorite.

— Which is our best. Let's go.

[Illusion Fight](/radio-nina/lutas-de-ilusao)

---

Nina adjusted the bass on her lap and opened on her phone the file Jack had sent on the way. The lyrics. The chords.

Nobody had ever asked Jack for the chords. Nobody had ever been there to ask.

She scrolled, went back, scrolled again. Her fingers went to the bass neck and started feeling out the notes without making sound, on the strings, like someone reading something under their breath.

One minute. Two.

Jack drummed on the rim of the drum box.

— So? Guys? Let's go? Is it gonna start or not? Pretty soon I'll be drunk.

---

Kim started slow.

A guitar riff. Dry, short, heavy, repeating. The pick scraping the low string, the left hand muting on the right beat.

And he was shy.

It was the first time. He realized it halfway through the first measure. All those years, in that garage, it had only been him and Jack. Nobody watching. Nobody listening, except Helena yelling from the living room to stop the noise.

Now there was someone.

He looked at Nina.

She was focused. Eyes on the bass neck, mouth moving a little, counting. She seemed to be taking it more seriously than he was. More seriously than Jack. He didn't understand why.

The riff went around. Again. And again. Three times more than it needed.

Jack slammed the drum box so hard the light bulb swung on its wire.

— JUST GO, DAMN IT! START ALREADY!

Kim closed his eyes.

And sang.

```letra
KIM: There's only one rule in a street fight,
KIM: There's only one rule in a street fight,
> The third time, Jack joins in.
KIM AND JACK: There's only one rule in a street fight...
```

Jack's drumsticks clicked together. One, two, three, four.

And the drums came in.

The torn snare cracked. The kick drum hit everyone in the chest. The dented cymbal sizzled. The hole where a tom should have been made Jack skip that beat and land on the next, and somehow that became the song's style.

```letra
JACK: Finding new weapons, we need to prepare,
JACK: Headed into the unknown, we're gonna throw ourselves in.

KIM: I've thought, I've tried, I've changed, I've returned,
KIM: Thinking about the infinite,
KIM: Finally found the enemy,

KIM AND JACK: GOOOOOO, ohhhh...
KIM AND JACK: And along the way...
```

And then came something neither of them expected.

---

The bass.

Low, fat, coming in underneath Kim's guitar and Jack's drums like ground appearing beneath someone who's falling. They both felt it in their bodies before they understood.

And the voice.

Nina opened her mouth and the garage changed size.

```letra
NINA: Tears fall when night falls,
NINA: Screams of despair rise above,
NINA: Hidden desires, repressed dreams,
NINA: Staring me in the face...
```

Kim opened his eyes and looked at her.

Jack looked at her.

Both impressed. Both without stopping playing. The music wouldn't let them stop.

It was Jack who took the helm. He always did. He started directing the music while playing, while singing, shouting over the noise, pointing the drumstick at whoever had to come in.

```letra
> Jack shouts: — Kim, your turn!
KIM: Face it!

JACK: But if I fall, I'll get back up,
> — Kim, go!
KIM: If I'm with you, I can change everything,
JACK: I'll never let your fear guide me,
> Kim comes in without Jack telling him.
KIM: Since I already took the first step, I'll keep going.

> — Nina, your turn!
NINA: With me it will always be like this,
> — Everybody!
ALL: I'll run
> Just Kim. Jack shouts, and he goes.
KIM: and fight until the end!
```

Jack took a breath and let out a voice nobody knew where it came from. Torn, guttural, from the bottom of his stomach.

```letra
JACK: Time to fight, time to fight, time to fight!
> — Everybody, quick stop! Go, Kim!
KIM: Illusion Fight
> — Everybody back in playing!
ALL: guided my heart,
> Nina takes over.
NINA: Gave me direction
> — Your turn, Kim, damn it! With feeling, fuck! Nina, trade off with me on power!
KIM: In search of power,
JACK: power,
NINA: power!
```

---

Something was happening in that garage.

Jack felt it. He was hitting harder than he knew how. Kim was playing faster than he knew how to play. Nina was laughing in the middle of a bass line, without missing a note.

The three of them kept looking at each other and nobody needed to say anything. Each one knew where the other was going to come in a second before they did. It was that thing Kim and Jack had in a fight. Only now there were three.

Nina led the chorus.

```letra
NINA: Illusion Fight in another dimension,
NINA: Frees my being...
> Kim comes in alone.
KIM: What I want is to win, win, win!
```

And Nina saw.

Kim was smiling.

Eyes closed, head low over the guitar, pick flying, and smiling. Not the half-smile of someone about to hit somebody. A full, open, childlike smile. A smile she was sure nobody at that school had ever seen.

She looked at Jack.

Jack was already looking at her. He winked.

*That's it.* She understood. *That's the thing he does and doesn't realize.*

---

Jack raised a drumstick.

— This one's mine. Nobody gets in the way.

And the rap came.

```letra
JACK: Broken, thought out, destroyed, carried,
JACK: My true power is believing
JACK: That I can face everything.
JACK: And if you're by my side,
JACK: Nobody will ever stop us! Ah, ah, ah,
JACK: So hold my hand,
JACK: Let's make some trouble
JACK: That will be remembered,
JACK: Lived and told
JACK: Until the end of time, forever...
JACK: Our names will always be spoken!

JACK: It's time, yeah...
```

— KIM, TEAR IT UP ON THE SOLO!

Kim tore it up.

Fast. Dirty. The pick going down and up at a speed the old guitar could barely handle, the one tuning peg different from the others groaning, one note ripping into the next.

— DESTROY! DESTROY! — Jack. — NINA, JUST KEEP THE BEAT! LET'S GO FASTER!

Faster.

More.

And they stopped.

All together, on the same beat, without anyone counting. Silence fell on the garage with the weight of a cymbal someone grabbed with their hand.

One second.

---

Kim came back in calm. Almost whispering. And Nina answered from the other side.

```letra
KIM: But if I fall, I'll get back up,
NINA: If I'm with you, I can change everything,
KIM: I'll never let your fear guide me,
NINA: Since I already took the first step, I'll keep going.
NINA: With me it will always be like this,
KIM: I'll run and fight until the end!
```

— Drums and vocals only! — Jack. — Come on, guys!

```letra
> Guitar and bass stop. Only kick drum, torn snare, and three voices.
ALL: Illusion Fight guided my heart,
ALL: Gave me direction,
ALL: In search of power, power, power!
```

— EVERYBODY BACK IN! HEAVY ENDING! LET'S CLOSE THIS SHIT OUT WITH A BANG!

```letra
ALL: Illusion Fight in another dimension,
ALL: Frees my being...
ALL: What I want is to win, win, win!

ALL: Illusion Fight guided my heart,
ALL: Gave me direction,
ALL: In search of power, power, power!

ALL: Illusion Fight in another dimension,
ALL: Frees my being...
ALL: What I want is to win, win, win!

ALL: Illusion Fight guided my heart,
ALL: Gave me direction,
ALL: In search of power, power, power!

ALL: Illusion Fight in another dimension,
ALL: Frees my being...
ALL: What I want is to win, win, win!
```

— Done.

Jack grabbed the cymbals with his hand.

And only Kim's guitar was left. The same riff from the beginning. Dry. Short. Heavy.

```letra
KIM: There's only one rule in a street fight,
KIM: There's only one rule in a street fight,
KIM AND JACK: There's only one rule in a street fight...
```

---

The last chord hung in the air until it died out on its own.

Nobody said anything.

The light bulb still swung on its wire. Sweat dripped down Jack's forehead. Nina's fingers were red from pulling the thick string so much. Kim stood still with an open hand over the strings, muting them, looking at the floor.

The three looked at each other. Haaa. Out of breath, all three.

— Did you guys feel that? — Nina. She was laughing, and didn't know why. — Jack. How did you manage to coordinate a song like that? How did you know who was going to come in where?

Jack spun a drumstick between his fingers.

— I didn't coordinate anything, man. — He said, serious, in a way that wasn't very Jack. — The music was there. It has its own power. I was just showing you guys what it wanted us to do.

He looked at Kim. Looked at Nina.

— And it was sick with the three of us. With the three of us it became another song.

Kim said nothing.

He hung the guitar back on the nail. Slowly. The way you put away something that matters.

---

It was already night when they left.

The gas station convenience store was on the way to the base. Too-white light, fridge humming, a clerk behind the counter who didn't even look up from his phone when the door went ding-dong.

The three walked in laughing. Nina was still laughing about rehearsal. Kim and Jack bumped elbows in the snack aisle for no reason at all.

Then the two stopped laughing at the same time.

At the back of the store, near the coolers, there were two guys. One in a black hoodie. The other bald, tattoo crawling up his neck. They weren't looking at shelves. They were watching the store.

Kim and Jack looked at each other. Nudge. An elbow bump, this time in a different way.

— Get that cheese one. — Nina, pointing at a bag, without having noticed anything.

Jack grabbed it. Kim started walking slowly toward the back cooler.

---

Ding-dong.

Another one walked in. Cap, Argentina jersey, hand in his pocket.

At the back, the two pulled balaclavas over their heads. The bald one lifted his shirt and the gun showed at his waist.

The hooded one went straight to the register.

— Stick 'em up! Nobody move!

The clerk raised his hands. The phone fell on the counter.

Kim, behind the glass door of the cooler, looked at Jack.

Jack already had a soda can in his hand.

He threw it. Not at the robber. Down the aisle, high, spinning.

Kim took two steps and kicked the can in midair.

Wham.

The can flew across the store and burst in the hooded guy's face. Clank. He went backward, the gun slid across the floor under the shelf.

---

The bald guy fired.

Bang. Bang.

Bottles exploding on the shelf, glass flying. Kim was already on the floor, over the clerk, pushing the kid's head under the counter.

Nina and Jack dropped crouched behind the snack shelf.

Jack looked to the side. In the other aisle, also crouched, was the guy in the Argentina jersey.

— So, man. You gonna start something or what?

— What? What are you talking about?

— Cap, hand in pocket, came in together. I know you're with them.

— Are you crazy? I came to buy gum.

— Gum is at the register. — Jack, calm. — You're in the pet food aisle.

The guy looked around. Dog food bags on both sides.

And pulled the pistol from his waist.

---

Kim saw from where he was.

He grabbed the first thing on the shelf. A five-kilo bag of flour. He threw it over the aisles.

The bald guy shot at the bag.

Poof.

The whole store went white. A cloud of flour in the air, covering lights, shelves, people. From the middle of it, coughing. From both sides.

From the middle of the white, Jack's voice, calm:

— So, guys. Last chance to walk out of here in one piece. If you don't take it, things are gonna get different.

— Show yourself, you bastard!

The bald guy waved the gun everywhere. Couldn't see anything.

— Good. — Jack. — We gave you guys a chance.

---

Kim came out of the flour low.

Crouched, already inside the bald guy's guard. His fist came up from the floor to the guy's chin.

Thok.

The bald guy came off the floor. The gun fell among the scattered snacks.

The hooded guy tried to get up. Jack came from behind, grabbed his arm and twisted it all the way. Crack. The scream came with it.

The Argentina jersey guy came out of the pet food aisle shooting wildly. Nina swept his legs, and when he fell, a kick. Jack finished with a knee to the gut. Whump.

The flour settled slowly.

Three guys on the floor. Three guns far away. A clerk covered in flour, still under the counter.

---

Jack slapped the flour out of his green hair. He grabbed a bottle of vodka from the shelf behind the register and a pack of cigarettes, and set them on the counter.

The clerk stood up slowly.

— Hey, man. — Jack. — We're not gonna stick around to wait for the police. We don't really like police. Can you ring this up?

The guy looked at the three guys on the floor. Looked at Jack.

He rang it up. Beep.

Kim paid. Nina grabbed the cheese snack, which had survived.

The three walked out through the station lot. The bag swinging in Jack's hand. The dark street ahead.
