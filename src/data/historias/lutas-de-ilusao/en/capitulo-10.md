# CHAPTER 10 — Illusion Fight

Kim picked up the guitar again and started tuning.

He'd turn a tuning peg, pluck a string, listen, turn it again. No tuner, no app. By ear.

Nina walked around the garage. In the corner, behind the cardboard boxes, leaning against the wall, there was another instrument. Longer than the guitar. Four thick strings.

— Is this a bass?

— Yeah. — Jack, without taking his eyes off the snare drum, which he was tightening with his hand. — I grabbed that because I thought the second thing I was going to learn in life was bass. Because this band is always missing a bassist. — He pointed at Kim with a drumstick. — This guy's been saying that since we were twelve.

— I never said that.

— You say it with your eyes.

Nina ran her hand over the bass strings. A fine dust rose.

— You guys mind if I pick it up?

Jack and Kim looked at each other.

— Go ahead, mess with it. — Jack.

She picked up the bass, sat on the chair with the mended leg, and started tuning. Kim heard the first string. Heard the second. Stopped messing with the guitar for a second.

She was tuning by ear too.

---

— Ready. — Kim, adjusting the strap on his shoulder. — It's tuned. Which one you want to pull out, man?

Jack spun both drumsticks at the same time.

— Let's open with *Illusion Fight*.

Kim looked at him.

— Dude. Seriously. This can't be real. — He pointed his pick at Jack. — You're a son of a bitch. After everything I went through today. After what happened in that arena. You want to open with *Illusion Fight*.

— It's our best one.

— It's your favorite.

— Which is our best one. Let's go.

[Illusion Fight](/radio-nina/lutas-de-ilusao)

---

— Do you guys have the chord chart for this? — Nina, adjusting the bass on her lap. — The lyrics?

Kim and Jack looked at each other again.

No one had ever asked for the chord chart. No one had ever been there to ask.

— I'm writing it down. — Jack, already pulling his phone out of his pocket. — Hold on. I've got it all in a file. I'll send you the link.

Nina's phone buzzed. She opened it, scrolled, went back, scrolled again. Her fingers went to the bass neck and started finding the notes without making a sound, on top of the strings, like someone reading something under their breath.

One minute. Two.

Jack drummed on the rim of the snare.

— So, people? Let's go? Is it gonna start or not? Pretty soon I'm gonna be drunk.

---

Kim started slow.

A guitar riff. Dry, short, heavy, repeating. The pick scraping the low string, his left hand muting in perfect time.

And he was shy.

It was the first time. He realized it in the middle of the first measure. All those years, in that garage, it had only been him and Jack playing. No one watching. No one listening, except Helena yelling from the living room to stop with the noise.

Now there was someone.

He looked at Nina.

She was focused. Eyes on the bass neck, her mouth moving a little, counting. She seemed to be taking it more seriously than him. More seriously than Jack. He didn't understand why.

The riff looped. Again. And again. Three times more than it needed to.

Jack whacked the snare so hard the lightbulb swung on its wire.

— GO ALREADY, DAMMIT! START ALREADY!

Kim closed his eyes.

And sang.

```letra
KIM: Só existe uma regra na briga de rua,
KIM: Só existe uma regra na briga de rua,
> Na terceira vez, o Jack entra junto.
KIM E JACK: Só existe uma regra na briga de rua...
```

Jack's sticks hit each other. One, two, three, four.

And the drums came in.

The torn snare cracked. The kick drum hit everyone in the chest. The dented cymbal sizzled. The hole where a tom should have been made Jack skip that beat and land on the next, and somehow that became the way the song went.

```letra
JACK: Encontrando novas armas, precisamos nos preparar,
JACK: Em direção ao desconhecido, vamos nos jogar.

KIM: Já pensei, já tentei, já mudei, já retornei,
KIM: Pensando no infinito,
KIM: Finalmente achei o inimigo,

KIM E JACK: GOOOOOO, ohhhh...
KIM E JACK: E no meio do caminho...
```

And then came something neither of them expected.

---

The bass.

Low, fat, coming in under Kim's guitar and Jack's drums like ground appearing underneath someone who's falling. Both of them felt it in their bodies before understanding.

And the voice.

Nina opened her mouth and the garage changed size.

```letra
NINA: Lágrimas descem quando a noite cai,
NINA: Os gritos de desespero se sobressaem,
NINA: Desejos escondidos, sonhos reprimidos,
NINA: Me encaram de frente...
```

Kim opened his eyes and looked at her.

Jack looked at her.

Both impressed. Both without stopping. The music wouldn't let them stop.

It was Jack who took the helm. He always did. He started directing the song while playing, while singing, yelling over the noise, pointing his drumstick at whoever had to come in.

```letra
> O Jack grita: — Kim, sua vez!
KIM: Enfrente!

JACK: Mas, se eu cair, eu vou me levantar,
> — Kim, vai!
KIM: Se estou contigo, posso tudo mudar,
JACK: Jamais deixarei seu medo me guiar,
> O Kim entra sem o Jack mandar.
KIM: Se já dei o primeiro passo, vou continuar.

> — Nina, sua vez!
NINA: Comigo sempre será assim,
> — Todo mundo!
TODOS: Vou correr
> Só o Kim. O Jack grita, e ele vai.
KIM: e lutar até o fim!
```

Jack sucked in air and let out a voice nobody knew where it came from. Torn, guttural, from the bottom of his stomach.

```letra
JACK: Hora de lutar, hora de lutar, hora de lutar!
> — Todo mundo, parada rápida! Vai, Kim!
KIM: Lutas de Ilusão
> — Volta todo mundo tocando!
TODOS: guiou meu coração,
> A Nina assume.
NINA: Me deu a direção
> — Sua vez, Kim, porra! Com vontade, caralho! Nina, reveza comigo no poder!
KIM: Em busca de poder,
JACK: poder,
NINA: poder!
```

---

Something was happening in that garage.

Jack felt it. He was hitting harder than he ever hit. Kim was playing faster than he knew how to play. Nina was laughing in the middle of a bass line, without missing a single note.

The three of them looked at each other and nobody needed to say anything. Each one knew where the other was going to come in a second before they did. It was that thing Kim and Jack had in a fight. Except now there were three.

Nina led the chorus.

```letra
NINA: Lutas de Ilusão em outra dimensão,
NINA: Liberta o meu ser...
> O Kim entra sozinho.
KIM: O que eu quero é vencer, vencer, vencer!
```

And Nina saw.

Kim was smiling.

Eyes closed, head down over the guitar, pick flying, and smiling. Not the half-smile of someone about to hit someone. A whole, open, childlike smile. A smile she was sure nobody at that school had ever seen.

She looked at Jack.

Jack was already looking at her. He winked.

*That's it.* She understood. *That's the thing he does and doesn't realize.*

---

Jack raised a drumstick.

— This one's mine. Nobody interfere.

And the rap came.

```letra
JACK: Partido, pensado, destruído, carregado,
JACK: O meu verdadeiro poder é acreditar
JACK: Que tudo posso enfrentar.
JACK: E, se ao meu lado você está,
JACK: Ninguém jamais poderá nos parar! Ah, ah, ah,
JACK: Então segura a minha mão,
JACK: Vamos criar uma confusão
JACK: Que será lembrada,
JACK: Vivida e contada
JACK: Até o fim dos tempos, eternizados...
JACK: Nossos nomes sempre serão falados!

JACK: Tá na hora, é...
```

— KIM, TEAR UP THE SOLO!

Kim tore it up.

Fast. Dirty. The pick going down and up at a speed the old guitar could barely handle, the tuning peg that was different from the others groaning, one note tearing into the next.

— DESTROY! DESTROY! — Jack. — NINA, JUST KEEP THE BEAT! LET'S GO FASTER!

Faster.

More.

And they stopped.

All together, at the same time, without anyone counting. Silence fell on the garage with the weight of a cymbal someone grabbed with their hand.

One second.

---

Kim came back calm. Almost whispering. And Nina answered from the other side.

```letra
KIM: Mas, se eu cair, eu vou me levantar,
NINA: Se estou contigo, posso tudo mudar,
KIM: Jamais deixarei o seu medo me guiar,
NINA: Se já dei o primeiro passo, vou continuar.
NINA: Comigo sempre será assim,
KIM: Vou correr e lutar até o fim!
```

— Just drums and vocals! — Jack. — Come on, guys!

```letra
> A guitarra e o baixo param. Só o bumbo, a caixa rasgada e três vozes.
TODOS: Lutas de Ilusão guiou meu coração,
TODOS: Me deu a direção,
TODOS: Em busca de poder, poder, poder!
```

— EVERYBODY BACK IN! HEAVY ENDING! LET'S CLOSE THIS SHIT OUT WITH A BANG!

```letra
TODOS: Lutas de Ilusão em outra dimensão,
TODOS: Liberta o meu ser...
TODOS: O que eu quero é vencer, vencer, vencer!

TODOS: Lutas de Ilusão guiou meu coração,
TODOS: Me deu a direção,
TODOS: Em busca de poder, poder, poder!

TODOS: Lutas de Ilusão em outra dimensão,
TODOS: Liberta o meu ser...
TODOS: O que eu quero é vencer, vencer, vencer!

TODOS: Lutas de Ilusão guiou meu coração,
TODOS: Me deu a direção,
TODOS: Em busca de poder, poder, poder!

TODOS: Lutas de Ilusão em outra dimensão,
TODOS: Liberta o meu ser...
TODOS: O que eu quero é vencer, vencer, vencer!
```

— Done.

Jack grabbed the cymbals with his hand.

And only Kim's guitar was left. The same riff from the beginning. Dry. Short. Heavy.

```letra
KIM: Só existe uma regra na briga de rua,
KIM: Só existe uma regra na briga de rua,
KIM E JACK: Só existe uma regra na briga de rua...
```

---

The last chord hung in the air until it died on its own.

Nobody said anything.

The lightbulb was still swinging on its wire. Sweat was dripping down Jack's forehead. Nina's fingers were red from pulling the thick string so much. Kim was standing still with his open hand over the strings, muting them, looking at the floor.

The three of them looked at each other.

— Bro. — Jack, out of breath. — That was fucking awesome.

— That was incredible. — Nina. She was laughing, and didn't know why. — Jack. How did you manage to coordinate a song like that? How did you know who was going to come in where?

Jack spun a drumstick between his fingers.

— I didn't coordinate anything, man. — Him, serious, in a way that wasn't very Jack. — The song was there. It has its own power. I was just showing you what it wanted us to do.

He looked at Kim. Looked at Nina.

— And it got fucking awesome with the three of us. With the three of us it became a different song.

Kim didn't say anything.

Hung the guitar back on the nail. Slowly. The way you put away something that matters.
