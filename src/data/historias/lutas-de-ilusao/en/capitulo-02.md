# CHAPTER 2 — The Machine and the Rose

The Elite Academy room didn’t look like a classroom.

The walls responded to touch. The desks had tactile surfaces. The augmented reality glasses waited in their stands as if they were breathing.

The Machine was already there.

Tall, slender, charcoal-gray suit without a single crease out of place, glasses on, standing in front of the holographic panel. He watched the two late students walk in and said nothing.

Kim and Jack found their usual seats and put on the glasses. Their posture shifted for the second time that morning. Here it wasn’t just the uniform. It was real focus.

Outside that room they were other people. Inside, they were what Osvaldo had bet they would be.

The Machine turned his back to the class, facing the panel.

— Let’s start today’s lesson.

The door opened.

*Zzzt.*

The holographic panel flickered. The diagrams split into bands, the globes blinked, the whole image froze and dissolved into noise. In the middle of the interface, in red letters:

**ERROR**

The Machine looked at the panel. Looked at the door. Kim looked. Jack looked, and something in his eyes melted on the spot.

A girl with pink hair crossed the room through the malfunction, as if the glitch belonged to her.

The Machine raised his pen.

— Name.

— Nina.

She said it with a voice that didn’t ask permission. She adjusted the strap of her black leather backpack and walked to the teacher, holding out her wrist where the smartwatch waited for scanning. The blue light validated her enrollment with a beep.

The Machine pointed to the empty seat without taking his eyes off the equations in the air.

![](/assets/images/livro/02.png)

Nina scanned the room before moving. There was no nervous curiosity in that look. There was assessment. Every face, every posture, every detail being filed in order of relevance. The empty seat was next to Jack.

She walked over and settled in with an economy of movement that made even taking the tablet out of her backpack something calculated.

Jack stopped breathing at a normal rhythm.

Kim registered that without turning his head.

— Again...

---

Jack wrote something in the corner of the tablet and slid it discreetly toward Kim.

*My dude, it’s over, I’m done, I’m in love, I found the woman of my entire life, our kids are gonna be stylish and beautiful.*

Kim read it. Slowly raised a look of contempt at Jack and, still looking at him, typed.

*Holy shit, every week this same shit, Jack. Focus on the damn exercise.*

Jack typed back immediately.

*But it’s this one, this is the one, pink hair, gorgeous, goddess attitude, she owns my heart.*

The Machine turned to the class with a direct question about the problem in the air. Jack raised his hand before Kim even finished calculating the answer. He answered correctly. The Machine nodded with the economy of someone confirming the obvious and returned to the equations.

Jack leaned back with his hands behind his head and the satisfaction of someone who knows he left his mark.

*Did she notice? Besides being gorgeous and wonderful, I’m very intelligent.*

*How can this son of a bitch be so fast?*, Kim thought.

Nina hadn’t looked at him once.

---

The bell between classes rang.

The Machine gathered his materials with the same precision with which he had taught and left without ceremony. The room exhaled. Kim closed the tablet. Jack spun in his chair with the smile of someone who has already decided what he’s going to do.

— Hi. I’m Jack. It’s going to be a pleasure for you to get to know me.

Nina didn’t take her eyes off the tablet.

— Maybe your small mind hasn’t noticed... I didn’t introduce myself precisely to avoid conversations like this.

Kim went absolutely still. He’d seen Jack get rejected before, but that acid and that fast was a new record.

Jack opened his mouth. Closed it. Opened it again.

— A-and I already told you, my name is J... Jack. Pleasure.

— You’re fucked, dumbass — Kim whispered, without moving his lips.

— I am N. i. n. a. — Each letter separate. Pause between them. — And I want to be left alone.

She went back to the tablet.

Jack’s silence had texture. It was the silence of someone who hit the ground from a height he didn’t expect and is still processing the impact.

Kim turned his face slowly toward his friend. Pointed at him with one finger, unhurried, without making a sound. Opened his phone and began typing something with the solemnity of someone documenting a historic event.

*JACK’S TROLL DIARY*

*#247 — Got rejected by the new girl.*

Jack looked at the screen. Looked at Kim. His expression closed.

— You’re not writing that down, are you...

Kim didn’t answer. Kept typing.

— Kim...

*Saved.*

He put the phone in his pocket.

The room had registered everything. Giggles rippled across the desks in a wave. Even the Machine, who was still at the door, seemed to let slip something that was almost expression before turning his back.

Jack tried to reformulate. His mouth opened.

Nina didn’t look up.

Jack closed his mouth.

*Way to go, man. Epic embarrassment, and right in front of Kim. He’ll never let me forget this.*

---

The classroom door opened.

It wasn’t the way a door opens between one class and another. It was the way a door opens when whoever’s coming in isn’t asking permission.

Freddy first. The black eye still throbbing, the school blazer buttoned all the way up, his finger already pointing.

— Those two right there!

Behind him, the guy in the leather jacket. Bald, broad shoulders, no uniform and no hurry. He swept the room with his eyes and stopped at the two desks.

— Blue cap and green hair. You two, come with me.

The room went quiet in the wrong way.

Jack didn’t even turn his head.

— Today is band practice, damn it! — Low, only for Kim, his eyes shining. — It’s gonna be fucking awesome!

— Get that out of your head. — Kim moved on to the next exercise on the tablet. — I have to work.

The two stayed right there. Sitting. Talking.

Nina looked up from the tablet for the first time since she’d sat down. Looked at the door. Looked at the faces of the other students, who didn’t look surprised at all. Followed the bald guy’s gaze to the two desks.

The two who kept talking as if there was no one at the door.

*Wow. Action on the very first day.*

She put the tablet in her backpack and waited.
