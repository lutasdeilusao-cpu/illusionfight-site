# CHAPTER 26 — Joaquim Leal Reis

Monday started like any Monday.

Jack and Kim met up, went to school, and found Nina. Class. The three of them studying seriously, Kim paying attention, Jack pretending, Nina for real.

When the bell for the last class rang and the three were packing up their things, the hallway made a different sound.

A buzz. People talking low and fast. People running to the window.

— Did you see who's out there? — A girl next to them, to her friend. — I can't believe he's here. What do you think he wants?

— He's way better looking in person. — Her friend, pressed to the glass. — You think he came to challenge someone?

Kim and Jack looked at each other.

— What are these people talking about? — Jack.

— No idea. — Nina. — But it looks like there's someone important outside. You think it's trouble for you guys?

— I don't think so. — Kim. — We had a quiet week. We didn't do anything. Must be something else.

They headed out through the hallway, trying to dodge the crowd gathering at the front door, to take another route to their favorite spot.

And then the school speaker crackled.

— Attention. Could Mr. Joaquim Leal Reis please come forward? We need to speak urgently with Mr. Joaquim Leal Reis.

Kim stopped in the middle of the hallway.

Lowered his head.

Jack started laughing. Not a laugh. A fit. Leaning against the wall, hand on his stomach, out of breath.

Nina looked from one to the other.

— Who's Joaquim?

Jack couldn't answer. He just pointed at Kim.

Nina looked at Kim. Kim had the face of someone who wanted the hallway floor to open up and swallow him whole.

— Please. — The speaker again. — Mr. Joaquim Leal Reis. Present yourself immediately.

Kim picked up his pace.

Too late.

— IT'S HIM! — Someone from his class. — JOAQUIM IS HIM! THERE!

Everyone turned.

---

In the middle of the crowd at the door, a corridor opened up.

And through it came a young man.

Asian features, narrow eyes. Blond hair, long, almost to his shoulders. A white hoodie, black details on the zipper and pockets. White jeans. He walked with his hands in his pockets, slowly, with the air of someone who's used to being stared at wherever he goes and stopped caring long ago.

Behind him, a quiet young man carrying a padded bag.

The blond young man stopped in front of Kim. Took one hand out of his pocket.

— Hello. You must be Kim. The guy who impressed me. — A calm voice, with a light accent. — I'm Shuntaro Chirasu. Position nine hundred ninety-eight in the world ranking of Illusion Fight. It's a pleasure to meet you.

He gave a bow. Short, natural, the way it's done in his country. And held out his hand.

Kim looked at Jack. Looked at Nina. He didn't understand anything. But he shook his hand.

Shuntaro smiled.

— I came from very far away. From Azuma. Just to fight you. It was nine hours of travel, and I'm really looking forward to this fight. — Pause. — Would you do me that honor, Mr. Joaquim?

Jack had another fit.

---

Kim shrugged.

It had been a while since his last LDI fight. He turned to the two of them.

— So? What do you guys think? I'm not really getting what's going on. But it looks like this guy wants to fight. And you know I don't back down from a fight.

Nina had her phone in her hand, screen toward her, and her face changed.

— Kim. I don't know if that's a good idea. — Low. — I think you should fight people a little above you. Not way above. This guy is famous. I lived four years in Azuma. Over there he's like... like a celebrity. He's been in LDI for six months. Six. And he's already in the top thousand in the world. He knows exactly what he's doing. That's my opinion.

Jack, recovered, wiped his eye.

— Nina. If you're trying to convince Kim not to fight, that's the worst possible strategy. Saying the guy is really strong, really famous, really dangerous, only makes him want it more. — He looked at Kim. — What I can tell you, Kim, is the chances of you taking the worst beating of your life are high. But I don't decide for you. If you want to get beat up, go. The guy's good.

Kim looked at Shuntaro.

He read the face. The posture. The way he held his shoulders. There was respect there. It wasn't a provocation. And there was also the calm certainty of someone who knows he's stronger and doesn't need to say it.

— Call me Kim. — Kim. — And another thing. You came from very far away expecting to get something. I don't think you're stupid enough to spend nine hours traveling to challenge just anyone. If you think it's worth fighting me, then let's fight.

Shuntaro genuinely smiled for the first time.

The hallway exploded.

Nobody at that school had ever seen a live LDI fight from someone in the top thousand. Phones going up everywhere. People running to tell other people.

---

Right there, in front of the school, the crowd opened a circle.

Shuntaro called his assistant, who opened the padded bag and took out two SBIs. One of them was clearly on another level. You could see it in the finish, the weight, the light running along the edge.

Kim sat on the ground against the school wall and took his out of his backpack. Jack and Nina helped him fit it on.

— Be careful. — Nina, adjusting the strap. — He uses his power output really well. Great technique. He's a strategist. He'll analyze you a lot before doing anything. If you can finish it fast, finish it. Don't let the fight drag. The truth is your chances of winning are small. Given how much you've trained.

— Relax, girl. — Kim. — Don't worry. If I lose, I lose. What can you do. The fun is in trying. From what you guys are saying, he's way stronger than me. If I win, I'll move up a few spots in the ranking. — Pause. — And besides Freddy's bet, this game hasn't paid me a cent. Maybe by moving up I'll start earning something. In the end that's what matters, right?

— You're completely right, man. — Jack. — Until it pays for the egg sandwich, it ain't worth it. So let's go get that egg sandwich.

Jack sat down next to Kim with his SBI in his hand. Nina sat on the other side.

— We're going to watch from inside. — Jack.

---

On the other side of the circle, Shuntaro was already sitting, SBI in hand, assistant beside him.

He looked at Kim over the crowd.

— I hope we have a good fight. — Shuntaro. — But I didn't come this far to lose. Get ready.

— There's no reason for me to start a fight if it's not to win. — Kim. — I don't get into fights to lose.

Shuntaro nodded. Satisfied.

The two slotted their SBIs in at the same time.

And the front of Elite Academy, packed with people, stood watching two boys sitting on the ground, eyes closed, not moving.
