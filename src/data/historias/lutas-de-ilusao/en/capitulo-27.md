# CHAPTER 27 — Ae Katu

The arena was the same as the first fight. Only bigger.

The stands rose in layers Kim didn't remember seeing, avatars of every kind, every size, every color, piled on top of each other. The crimson sky. The cities floating in slow orbit. And the noise.

The noise was different.

Nobody there was there because of Kim. The crowd, even bigger than the first time, was chanting one name.

*SHUN-TA-RO. SHUN-TA-RO.*

The new darling of Illusion Fight.

Kim was falling. And this time he wasn't thinking about the ground.

He landed in the arena like a cartoon hero. He touched down on one knee, one fist on the ground, and rose slowly in a pose that shut half the stands up.

And this time the look was different. His ranking had unlocked customization, and he had used it. Shirtless. A necklace on his chest. Eyes with a bluish tint. Thick beads on his wrists. Torn jeans. White sneakers. And on his head a cap with two words embroidered on the front.

*Ae katu.*

In an ancestral language, it meant power.

In the stands, in a small island of a different color, a king with a crown and green dreads raised both arms, fireworks bursting behind him.

— THAT'S IT, FUCK YEAH!

Next to the king, a girl with a short cape and fire hair didn't raise anything. She just smiled.

---

Shuntaro didn't fall from the sky.

On the other side of the arena, a giant gate opened with a boom, and he walked out through it. Custom entrance. Something that's only unlocked for those in the top thousand in the world. The crowd went wild.

His avatar looked like an ancient samurai. Focused expression. Hands in his pockets.

He reached the center of the arena, took his hands out of his pockets, and snapped his fingers. A yoyo appeared in his hand. He threw it, and the yoyo went down and up, and when it came up a thin thread of water came with it, spinning with it, shining.

Kim shrugged. He waited.

---

The Narrator rose out of the ground the usual way, his limbs too long, his purple suit bleeding into green, the bone microphone in his hand.

— LADIES AND GENTLEMEN! — The arena shook. — Today we have a gift! On the left side, the one who arrived without warning, the one who made the system forget to record, the one the arena called the Primordial! One win! Zero losses! Position one thousand four hundred twenty-three! BRAWLER! ONE! FOUR! TWO! FIVE! THREE! SIX!

Half the arena booed. The other half cheered. Nobody stayed quiet.

— On the right side! The boy from Azuma! Six months in LDI and not one month missed! Water Elemental! Position nine hundred ninety-eight! SHUNTARO CHIRASU!

The arena exploded.

— THE BETS ARE NOW OPEN!

The stands turned blue. Almost all of them. A sea of blue toward Shuntaro's side.

And a small island of another color in the middle of it. The king with the crown. The girl with the cape.

---

— He's using a yoyo. — Jack, in the stands. — And he still controls water. What do you think?

— You know what I think. — Nina. — I already said it. Kim is going to lose badly. He shouldn't be playing around with this guy.

— Nina. I get it. But you worry too much. Remember that when nobody expected it, he had already mastered weapon mode. Maybe he has a chance. Calm down, girl.

— I am calm. I'm just worried.

---

— Bets are closed. — The Narrator. — Let the Illusion Fight begin!

Shuntaro didn't move. He stood looking at Kim.

— You were impressive in your first fight. — He. — You got to one thousand two hundred and one. And then you dropped. Now you're at one thousand four hundred twenty-three. Because you haven't fought again even once. That shows you don't take this game as seriously as you should. — He spun the yoyo. — If you want to get up there, into the tournaments, I'm advising you. Not as an opponent. As a friend. Take LDI seriously.

Kim twisted his wrists. Cracked his fingers. Cracked his neck.

— Did we come here to fight or to keep chatting about moral lessons? What I do with my life is my business. Mind yours.

— I have nothing against you as a person. — Shuntaro, without changing his tone. — But as a player, I'm going to teach you something you won't forget. It's not easy being here.

He snapped his fingers. The yoyo stayed in his right hand, spinning. His left went back to his pocket.

And he started walking toward Kim. Slowly.

Kim walked too. Slowly. He opened his hand fast, and a small axe appeared in it.

*Calm down. Let's take it slow.*

---

Kim watched the yoyo.

It looked like a toy. A metal ball on a string. But he knew, from training with Vitória and Angel, that nobody should underestimate a weapon in LDI.

Maybe that was it. Maybe he looked at the yoyo too much.

Two steps away, Shuntaro advanced. But he left his right arm behind. Kim reacted to the arm coming at him. And he didn't see the left hand coming out of the pocket, already with another yoyo.

The second yoyo came into his stomach.

— Multiple Water Balls.

The two yoyos covered themselves with water spinning so fast they looked like blue energy balls. Kim jumped back to take the force out of the blow and spun on his foot, putting the small axe in front of his stomach.

— Dodge.

The yoyo changed direction in the air.

It dodged the small axe, went up, and hit Kim square in the chin.

The blow wasn't as strong as it looked. But the second yoyo was already coming, a second after the first, toward his face.

Kim raised his hand to defend.

— Tie.

The string wrapped.

Around his neck. And around the hand he had raised, pinning his hand against his neck. A thin, strong string, squeezing and cutting the skin.

And with the other yoyo, Shuntaro began.

— Hit. Hit. Hit.

One blow for each word. Ribs. Arm. Face. Stomach.

Kim was lost. The guy controlled the weapon with a precision that left no room for anything. No defense. No dodging. Each individual blow wasn't that strong. But one after another, without stopping, it started to take a toll.

His neck trapped. His hand trapped.

---

In the stands, Jack was laughing.

You couldn't tell if he was having fun or if he was nervous.

— That idiot fell for the guy's game! He's taking one after another! He better do something soon, or it's going to be a perfect!

Nina wasn't saying anything. She was watching Kim bleed. The welts rising. The cuts opening.

— This is what I was afraid of. — Her, low. — This guy didn't get where he is for nothing. Kim doesn't have the maturity yet to fight someone at that level. These guys know how to start and finish a fight.

Jack stopped laughing. He looked at her.

— Nina. I can't believe you've already given up on him.

— I haven't given up, I—

— You still don't understand Kim. Maybe this fight will show you. What Kim lacks in brains, he makes up for in stubbornness. He only shows his best when the worst happens. That's Kim. This fight is only starting and—

Down below, Kim swung his leg over the string that trapped his neck.

---

With the string under his knee, he gained slack. He forced his trapped hand against the string. Slipped his head through the loop.

And leapt far with everything he had.

He landed far. On one knee. One leg stretched out, trembling. He spat blood onto the arena floor. He started to get up.

— Like I was saying. — Jack, in the stands, settling into his seat. — The fight is only starting. And Kim is only warming up.

Shuntaro stood watching, a little surprised. He didn't expect Kim to get out of that. He fixed his hair calmly. Put his hands back in his pockets. Smiled.

— Impressive. Bravo. Congratulations on getting out of that. — He. — To be fair, you managed to position yourself to defend more than once. You have good instincts. That's why I chose to immobilize you.

Kim was breathing fast. He wiped his face. Looked at the blood on his hand. Looked at Shuntaro.

He smiled.

And slapped himself in the face.

— I screwed up. That pisses me off. — And he was really angry. — It's different fighting someone like this. Sorry for not taking you seriously. — He straightened up. — But I think in this exchange we've already talked enough. My answer is this: I don't take this game seriously. But I take any fight very seriously. It's time to show you that with my hands.

Shuntaro broke into a huge smile. He fidgeted in place, excited.

— Finally. So you're going to show that power. The one from your first fight. We're all waiting for it. Me and a lot of people up in the rankings who are watching today just for that. Show it. So I can beat you at your best.

Kim understood he was talking about the Primordial.

And he didn't remember how he had done that. He didn't know if he could do it again.

— Man. If you're talking about that Primordial... — Kim. — I have no idea how that happened. To be honest, I don't even remember.

Shuntaro's smile disappeared.

— What a shame. — He. — Your only chance to beat me was in that power. Without it, this fight is over.
