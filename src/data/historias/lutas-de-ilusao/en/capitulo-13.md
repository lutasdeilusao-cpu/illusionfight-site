# CHAPTER 13 — My Baby

Kim woke up, brushed his teeth, showered, got dressed.

When he opened the bedroom door, he stopped.

There was a smell in the house.

He sniffed the air like a dog, looking for where it came from. Good smell. Food smell. He walked through the house after it and noticed something else on the way: the house was tidy. Clean.

He reached the kitchen door.

Helena was at the stove. Music playing loud, out loud, not on headphones: *Man! I Feel Like A Woman*, by Shena Twuin, the country she loved. And she was dancing. Shaking her hips, singing along, flipping the eggs in the pan. Coffee with milk on the table. Juice. Cut fruit. The dishes washed.

Kim stood in the doorway, smiling, just watching.

The song ended. Helena turned and came face to face with him.

— What are you doing standing there, you nut? You almost gave me a heart attack!

Kim lowered his head, smiling.

— It was such an unusual scene that I decided not to disturb it.

She smiled too.

— Okay, enough chit-chat, come eat before you're late for school, my baby.

*My baby.*

Kim sat down. She put a plate in front of him and a very hot coffee with milk. Sat on the other side. And the two had breakfast together, talking.

In the middle of breakfast, Kim got serious.

— Mom. You don't have to do this every morning just because we had that conversation...

She looked at him, confused.

— You think I'm doing this because of our conversation? That now I want to be a good mother? Don't be silly, boy! I do what I want, when I want! Nobody's going to tell me what I have to do.

You say what you want, you hear what you don't. Kim stayed quiet and finished his coffee.

He grabbed his school stuff. And then came another unusual thing.

His mother came close, hugged him, and kissed his cheek.

— Have a good day, son. Try not to get in trouble at school. And come home safe.

Kim was a little embarrassed.

— Thanks, Mom. I'll try. But I'm not promising anything.

---

Jack was on the stairs outside.

And he had a confused look. He was used to hearing the shouting every morning from that house. What he had just heard through the door was something else.

— Hey, man, all good? What's up?

— Huh? What do you mean? I have no idea what you're talking about.

— I'm talking about that little speech: "have a good day, don't get in trouble, come home safe." Who's in there? The voice was your mom's. The attitude wasn't.

Kim looked down. Then up. A little embarrassed.

— Ah, man, it's such a long story... But we have to start working. Let's go, I'll tell you on the way.

---

And he told.

Between one bus and another, between one little bag of candy and another, Kim told everything. The night. The fight. The bottle. The whole story his mother had told.

On Jack's face he saw the same hatred he had felt that night. The same he still felt every time he thought about it.

The two were like that. One's suffering was the other's. A bond the size of brothers.

— Man. — Jack, on the last bus, voice low. — Being as honest as possible, I don't know what to say. Except I'm angry. Angrier than I've ever felt in my life. The worst guy we've ever faced doesn't come close. What I feel is hate for those sons of bitches who did that to you and your mom. — He looked out the window. — And I feel useless. Because I know there's nothing I can do to change it.

Kim understood. It was exactly what he was feeling.

And it was, almost word for word, what he had said to his mother.

---

When they got off near school, Nina was at the gate.

Waiting for them.

It was funny. One day. She had been there one day and already seemed like a friend of years. The three walked down the hall, telling what each had done after leaving the night before.

— Guys. — Jack was first. — After I left, I went home. And I swear, it felt like with every step the distance got bigger. When I arrived, my dad was at the door waiting for me. He had to give me the key because I lost mine. He looked at me and said straight: "You're drunk, aren't you?". I stalled, stalled, and in the end he laughed and said: "Don't you have anything to do tomorrow?". I said no, that I'd made some money and was off. Then he said: "Go take a shower to get that crazy look off your face, I left food ready." I showered, ate a big meal, watched a movie and crashed.

Kim and Nina laughed at the way he told it.

— My dad freaked. — Nina. — I got home late and drunk, on the first day of school. He's overprotective, so you can imagine the scolding. But I was so drunk I barely heard the lecture. I went straight to the shower and crashed. Only woke up in the middle of the night to eat something. In the morning he asked me not to do it again. — She shrugged. — But... I don't know.

---

The eyes in the hallway were all on them.

And in the middle of the hallway, standing, was the principal. Walter. Arms crossed. Foot tapping on the floor. Face of someone who hadn't slept from anger.

— Can one of you explain to me what happened yesterday in front of the school?

Jack scratched his chin and smiled.

— In front of the school? I have no idea what you're talking about, sir.

— That's right. — Kim. — If you give us a clue, maybe we'll remember. But nothing's coming now.

— Don't toy with my patience! I know you know what happened, because you were the authors of the incident.

Jack opened his arms.

— Authors? Incident?

— You'll have to be more specific. — Kim, same face.

Walter rubbed his face.

— Yesterday, after dismissal, you assaulted a schoolmate. And I want to know why.

— First of all, define "schoolmate." — Jack.

— Don't make me lose any more patience. A student was assaulted by you yesterday and I want to know why. It's not possible that you don't remember someone you attacked.

Jack was about to answer. Kim went first.

— Say the student's name first. To avoid confusion.

— Samuel. He and his friends were assaulted by you.

— Samuel, Samuel... — Kim. — Is he kind of fat?

Walter blinked.

— We don't use that word here, Kim. But yes, Samuel is a little heavyset.

Kim's face hardened.

— So you're talking about the kid who was beating someone else just for the pleasure of beating?

— About what happened before, I don't know. — Walter sidestepping. — What I know is that Samuel and the others came to me saying they were assaulted by you.

Jack opened his mouth again. Kim raised his hand and he closed it.

— The matter is simpler than it seems. Did we hit him inside the school?

— No, it wasn't inside the school, but...

— As you yourself started this conversation, it wasn't inside the school. Problems that happen outside, they solve with the police. You're not the police. — Kim looked at the hallway clock. — Now we're starting to be late. Do you have anything else to say?

Walter turned red.

— Be careful, you two. Very careful not to lose your scholarships. I will ensure that every student at this school is always safe. No matter the consequences.

And he walked away stomping.

Nina looked at the two. The two were still serious. Then, slowly, a smile started to spread on one's face and then the other's.

She understood. For them, that was just another normal day.

The three continued down the hall talking, without noticing that the whole school was looking at Kim with phones in hand. Everyone there knew that the famous Brawler of Illusion Fight was him.

---

The duo had become a trio.

But that wasn't going to be an ordinary day. Jack had plans for Kim. And he knew it wouldn't be easy to convince him. But if there was anyone in the world who could convince Kim to do what he didn't want, it was Jack.

Before they reached the classroom, Ryan passed by them. And came back.

— So, gentlemen. I want to thank you once again for what you did for me yesterday. — He looked at Nina. — And thank you too, miss.

The three looked at each other with the same smile.

— Here you are, Mr. Kim. — Jack, all polite, extending an invisible bottle. — The 1944 wine, Levu vintage.

— Thank you, my dear companion. — Kim, all formal. — I will enjoy this precious moment with the beautiful lady at my side.

— Oh, it's a great pleasure. — Nina, hand in front of her mouth, voice thin. — It will be an honor to savor this drink alongside two such refined gentlemen.

The three burst out laughing.

Ryan didn't quite understand. But he knew he was being teased. And he didn't mind. Those three had saved his skin.

He said goodbye. Took two steps. And came back.

— Oh, right. This is for you, Kim. Just a moment.

He knelt on the ground and opened his bag. Inside, a box wrapped in gift paper.

Kim looked sideways at him.

— Man. Stop right there. Don't even think about continuing.

The tone made Ryan freeze. Kneeling, hands on the box, paralyzed.

— I don't know what you think happened yesterday. But what I know is you don't owe me anything. Much less a gift.

Jack and Nina burst out laughing at Kim's face, who didn't know what to do with it.

— Ah, but if you don't accept it'll be a shame. — Ryan. — Because I already have an identical one. I'll have nothing to do with that one.

— And what would this gift be? — Jack.

— An SBI. I bought an SBI for Kim after I saw a video of him on the internet.

— What video? — Nina. — What are you talking about?

Ryan felt the three on top of him at once.

— I think I better start from the beginning. Okay?

The three nodded yes.

— Yesterday, after you guys helped me, I went home and checked PowWow. One of the most watched videos was of Kim fighting Thunderbolt. The fight was impressive and I wanted to see more. But... sorry to say this... I never saw you with an SBI. I figured you didn't play. Or maybe couldn't afford one. But your fight was so sensational that it would be a waste for us not to be able to see more. So I bought one for you. As thanks. But it's not charity. It's investment. You're a rookie and unknown in the game. If you fight again, I'll be notified. And I'll make a big bet on you. Because if you win, the return is very high.

Kim scratched his head.

— How do you know it was me? My name wasn't there.

— Man. — Jack, smiling. — Your avatar is exactly like you. So that whole anonymity idea, for anyone who already knows you, is over.

And he turned to Ryan.

— But wait. You said free SBI? Like, for free? Free?

Ryan nodded.

Jack clasped his hands in gratitude and hugged the boy.

— Then, sir, thank you very much for this kind gesture. Can I pick up the gift now?

Ryan stood up from the ground with the box. And Jack grabbed it before Kim had time to refuse.

---

Nina already had her phone in hand, on PowWow.

There was the video. The one she had seen live. Among the ten most watched in the world.

Until then, none of the three had looked up the videos of that fight. The night before, they'd gone drinking and come home drunk. Nina hadn't opened PowWow. Jack had crashed. And Kim just wanted to forget what had happened inside the game.

She showed it. The clips. The short videos. It wasn't just one video. There were dozens.

Kim saw his own face next to a huge view count.

Jack opened the widest smile in the world. His friend had blown up. Now everyone was going to see what he had always seen.

Kim grabbed Jack by the neck.

— So, you son of a bitch, you told me I could stay anonymous in this shit?

— Calm down, man. — Jack, voice of someone suffocating. — You can from the next fight. How was I supposed to know you'd blow up on the very first one? We'll figure it out, relax.

Ryan watched that without understanding. Maybe he was mistaken, but it seemed Kim was not at all happy about getting famous.

Well. Not his problem. He couldn't be late for cram school.

— Guys, sorry, but I need to go. I hope you like the gift. And that Kim keeps fighting. Your fights are really good. See you.

He hurried off to a car waiting for him.

The three stayed there. Jack still trapped in Kim's arm.

— Kim. — Nina, after a while. — Let him go.

Kim rolled his eyes and let go. Jack pulled the collar of his shirt, checking his throat.

— Man. You almost killed me this time. I was seeing stars.

— Stop being silly, you asshole. I didn't even squeeze right.

---

Jack shrugged and tore the gift paper off completely.

Limited edition. And it had something special: you could take it to an Illusion Fight Corporation store and customize it however you wanted. Name, lighting, colors.

— Man. — Jack, amazed. — This SBI is incredible! Way better than mine. And way better than what we were going to get with my plan.

— What plan?

— I was going to take you today after school to some stores, to see if anyone would trade PUBLI with you for an SBI. But even if it worked, they'd give you a cheap one or some busted one they had there. This one was a find, man.

Nina took the SBI from his hand and looked carefully.

— Jack's right. Limited edition. You can get authorized modification by CLI. And you can take it to any store and it's free. Congratulations.

Jack looked at Kim, all happy.

— Don't you want to trade yours for mine?

Kim scratched his chin.

— Alright. I'll keep yours. You keep that thing.

— No. — Nina, shaking her head. — You can't accept something like that. You just got it as a gift and you're going to give it to Jack? Don't be silly, Kim.

Kim breathed in to answer. Mid-breath, gave up. Lowered his head and, very grumpily, took the box from Jack's hand.

— Fine. Fine. You want me to keep it? I'll keep it. What can I do.

— Seriously? Sometimes you two are like two children.

— Okay, mom. — Jack. — Okay, mom. We promise to behave.

Nina laughed.

---

They walked on. Kim looking at the SBI closely for the first time. In his only fight, the one who had put the device on his face was Jack, and he was more worried about the opponent.

— What? — Jack. — Impressed?

— This looks expensive. How much does a thing like this cost?

— Ah, man, I paid about four thousand for mine. And mine is one of the cheapest.

— Holy shit, dude. And how much does this one cost?

— Yours should be around sixty, seventy thousand. — Nina.

Kim put his hand on his chest as if having a heart attack and held onto Jack.

— No. Seriously, guys. Stop. We have to return this. Can't keep it. For real! Bro, look at the value of this! No, no, no! We have to sort this out today. It's going to be trouble. It's going to be trouble!

Jack and Nina laughed heartily.

— Calm down, man! — Nina. — Why all this drama? To them that must be like pocket change. He said he already had an identical one. If he didn't want you to have it, he wouldn't have given it to you.

— But, bro, this is way too expensive! No one in their right mind gives this away for free. It doesn't exist! For sure he's going to want something in return. And I don't have patience for quid pro quo when I don't know the terms of the deal beforehand.

Jack knew his friend. He knew he wouldn't be at peace with it.

— Man, it's fine. It's fine. Today you use the device, and tomorrow we give it back to the guy. Okay?

Kim nodded.

They put the box away and went to class. The rest of the lessons passed with Kim trying to concentrate and Jack glancing at his friend's backpack now and then.

Only after the final bell did they leave school and head to a nearby square.
