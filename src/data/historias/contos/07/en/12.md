# CHAPTER 12 — The Panther

**KIM**

When I went in, I just looked around.

Searching. I didn't care about anything else. Just stayed sharp about the guns.

And then I found her.

In a corner. Looked like she was out. Drugged.

The woman with the purple hair.

Helena.

---

And, once again, something inside me broke.

I don't know why. But I felt an enormous happiness. A joy that didn't fit inside me, at having found Helena in the middle of all those guys.

There were a lot of people to break. A lot of people to take down.

But, at first, there was only one target.

---

**JACK**

As soon as we went in, I knew shit was about to go down. Because I saw Helena too.

She looked like she'd taken something. Her clothes were messed up. If they hadn't done anything yet, they were going to. It was written all over what was about to happen there.

There wasn't time.

On all fours, Kim charges twice as fast. He went past everybody and straight at the guy who looked like the big boss.

The guy opened his mouth. Couldn't tell if he was going to ask "who are you" or "what the fuck is this".

Kim jumped on him. The guy was bigger, so he wrapped him up mid-leap, pinning both of the guy's arms.

And started headbutting.

One. Another. Another. And laughing.

The smile opened, the blood ran, and the guy went down to his knees, eating headbutts.

---

It looked like Kim wanted to do something else to him.

But the others started arming up. You could hear the safeties coming off.

Obviously the shooting started.

---

I did what I could.

I grabbed the mini Cokes off my belt and started throwing. In one guy's face. In another's. At everybody.

Then I ripped open the baggies of powder and threw it over everybody, covering my nose. There were some ceiling fans on. The powder spread through the whole room. Then I ripped open the packages on the table too.

**KIM**

So that's what the powder was for.

**JACK**

I told you it was a priority.

---

In the middle of the chaos, I grabbed a wad of cash, poured the last of my bottle on it and set it on fire.

A shame. The booze, I mean.

The fire spread.

— You're burning money?! — somebody yelled. — Who the fuck are you, you sons of bitches? What's going on?

Powder in the air, money on fire, bullets everywhere. Shit was insane.

---

The problem was Kim.

He jumped from one side to the other. Every jump was a guy on the floor.

It was creepy. The room was like a warehouse, full of corners and partitions. The guys just vanished. They were there and, the next second, they weren't.

Kim moved low. Dragging. And leaped. And tore. And choked. And hit. And broke. You could hear bone.

And he was never satisfied.

---

There was one where Kim hooked his arm under the guy's leg. The leg went up.

And kept going up, until the guy's foot touched his shoulder.

That hurt. That one wasn't getting up again, for sure.

---

Let me explain something.

When we fight in serious mode, we want to make sure nobody comes back into the fight. Unconscious isn't a guarantee, because the guy can fake it. The surest way to take someone out of the fight is to break a bone.

It's not like the movies. With a broken bone, a person only thinks about the broken bone. They don't think about fighting anymore.

And, that night, yes, we enjoyed breaking some bones. Not everybody there deserved it. But that was the situation.

If you can't take the heat, stay out of the kitchen.
