# CHAPTER 11 — Official Version

— …and nobody ever proved a thing — I wrapped up, and took a sip from my cup to give the audience time to let it sink in.

The parking lot went quiet. The Beetle with no wheels, the pickup with a tree in the engine, the half-empty bottle on the hood ([main story, ch. 9](/historias/lutas-de-ilusao/capitulo-09)).

Nina had her cup frozen halfway to her mouth.

— So Freddy's black eye…

— Was a knee that wasn't ours. Tragic accident.

— And Jasmine.

— Between the couple.

She looked at Kim. Kim was leaning on the hood, smoking, with the face of someone who'd just heard his own life narrated by somebody else.

— Jack. — Kim let the smoke out slowly. — That's not exactly how it happened.

— Yes, it is.

— And it wasn't a little pastry. It was a stuffed chicken croquette. I remember it clearly.

— It was a little pastry, Kim. With croquette filling, if that makes you happier.

— And I didn't say "worth it."

— You said it with your eyes, Kim. I translated.

— And it wasn't a slice of cake, it was…

— Doesn't matter. — I raised my hand. — Doesn't matter. This is how it is now. This is how people are going to hear it.

Kim opened his mouth. Thought. Closed it again and picked up his cup.

He knows when he's lost an argument. It's one of his talents.

---

Nina laughed. That laugh she tries to hold back and can't.

— You two are unbelievable.

— Thank you.

— That wasn't a compliment.

— I decide what's a compliment.

---

And then I turned.

Not to Nina. Not to Kim.

To you.

Yeah, you. Reading this right now, who knows where, who knows what time, probably when you should be doing something else.

The side character is going to say it didn't go quite like that. He'll say I exaggerated. He'll say there were fewer people, that the knee was different, that he "wasn't that worried about the cake."

He's lying. It's opposition scheming.

That's exactly how it happened.

And now that you've read it, you're a witness too.
