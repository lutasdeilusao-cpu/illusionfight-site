# CHAPTER 5 — IN THE HEART OF SHADOWS

### NARRATOR: MINUS

The chain pulses on my right arm.
 Electric blue. Links slithering from palm to shoulder. Alive. Waiting.

Three blocks south. Luna runs through the streets of Brasília, terror written into every step.

— “How many?” I whisper.

Nefeli floats beside me. A silver smoke screen materializes:

— SEVEN SHADOWS. TWO ILLUMINATED.

I close my hand. The chain tightens around my arm.

Two Illuminated. A Special Night. Orchestrating. Making sure this time she doesn’t escape.

But this time…

— “Red Eyes. Delta Protocol.”

The creature materializes to my left. Gray-and-black mist shaped like a canine. Small — the size of a stray dog. One torn ear. Three translucent legs. The eyes — the only solid points in the entire ethereal body — glow vivid red.

It doesn’t bark. Doesn’t growl. It just pulses once.

Understood.

Four years training this communication. Four years turning a wild shadow-dog into an ally.

I still remember the first command it obeyed. Six months of attempts.

But now…

Now it’s an extension of me.

Below, Luna turns into a narrower street. The shadows follow, gaining form.

And above — always above — the two Illuminated float. Golden light pulsing against the night sky.

My hand opens. The chain slides, serpents through the air.

— “Let’s go.”

### NARRATOR: NEFELI

I see when Minus moves.

He doesn’t run. He flies.

The chain lashes out, anchoring to a streetlight three buildings ahead. His body is pulled through the air — a movement practiced a thousand times in the abandoned ruins of Ceilândia.

Red Eyes follows. It has already received its orders, just as it was trained. It doesn’t seem to have emotions like the old Shadow. That’s why Minus gave it a new name. Red Eyes wasn’t a friend.

It was a soldier.

Gray-and-black mist glides beside me, invisible to the impure but solid enough for me.

Through the cord — that connection between me and Minus that only exists when we need it — I feel it.

Determination. Controlled rage. And something deeper.

Curiosity.

He wants to know. To understand. Why don’t other marked ones fight? Why did only he develop power?

The girl — Luna — might have answers.

Or she might die trying.

Both outcomes serve a purpose.

I project the screen:

— SHE’S ENTERING THE ZONE.

Minus doesn’t respond. He already knows. Delta Protocol will be followed to the end.

Two blocks. One block.

The shadows surround Luna. She backs away, her shield pulsing weakly. Cracked. About to shatter completely.

And then Minus acts.

### NARRATOR: MINUS

I reach an open area. Tall buildings on both sides. I know this place — Setor Comercial Sul, near Conjunto Nacional. During the day, thousands of people. Now, almost empty. Just a few hurried pedestrians who see nothing beyond normal shadows.

Unfortunately, still busy. Even at night, there are witnesses. But I have no choice.

I leap from the rooftop.

The chain extends — twenty meters of blue-and-black links whipping through the air. The end narrows, condenses, becomes a sharp point.

I don’t aim at the shadows around Luna.

I aim up.

At the Illuminated.

Finally, I can test my power against them. I have to know if it works.

The chain fires. It cuts through the air like lightning. The sound is amplified whip-crack.

The first Illuminated moves.

For the first time, I see them move. And it’s completely different from what I expected.

There is no transition. No gradual motion. The golden form simply isn’t there anymore.

And reappears three meters to the left.

The chain passes where its shoulder should have been. It doesn’t hit. Not even close.

It’s too fast.

Shit.

The chain retracts. Searches for an anchor. Latches onto the edge of a building.

I stabilize. Feet hit the wall. Push. Jump again.

This time higher. Closer.

The chain lashes — not a direct attack. A circular motion. Surrounding.

The Illuminated notices. The golden form turns.

And for the first time…

Recognition.

You.

Not a voice. A feeling. Pure hatred crossing the air like a pressure wave.

The broken one who dared to touch. Who escaped shadows, the impure, even the Shadow World.

The error that still exists.

Golden light pulses. Stronger. Condensed rage.

But it doesn’t attack.

It just watches.

As always.

My hand closes. The chain responds.

“Let’s see if you only watch when I do this.”

I spin in the air. The free chain coils around my body, gaining speed. Faster. Faster.

Ten meters from the Illuminated. Five. Three.

— “TOTAL PRISON!”

The chain explodes into motion. No longer contained.

Twenty lines of blue-and-black links whip in all directions. Crossing. Tangling. Like a snake wrapping its prey.

Around the golden form. Neck. Chest. Arms.

Tightening.

Got you! So my power can touch you!

The Illuminated freezes.

For a fraction of a second.

Then the free hand — the only one the chain didn’t fully bind — moves.

And closes around the links.

Grabs them.

### NARRATOR: NEFELI

The Illuminated doesn’t move.

It doesn’t need to.

It simply holds the chain. Firm. Like an adult holding a child’s toy.

And then it pulls.

Minus is ripped out of the air. His body whips like a ragdoll. Slams into a glass building wall. The impact cracks the surface. Then into the adjacent rooftop. Concrete shatters.

Through the cord, I feel it.

Pain. Shock. And something worse.

He’s too strong.

Far too strong.

Four years of training. Four years of preparation. And still…

Still not enough.

The Illuminated casually moves its arm — almost bored — slamming Minus against walls and rooftops as if he weighs nothing.

It doesn’t let go. Just holds him. Impassive. Demonstrating with brutal clarity:

You are not a threat.
 You are an inconvenience.

Then something changes.

The second Illuminated — until now only watching — raises what might be an arm.

And points downward.

At the shadows surrounding Luna.

An order.

### THE SHADOWS OBEY

They move.

No longer circling slowly. No longer testing.

Attacking.

Seven shadows explode into motion.

But they are no longer humanoid shapes.

They mutate.

The first splits in half. An impossible spine snaps. The upper half becomes a torso with four arms. The lower half becomes a crawling mass of twisted legs. Two bodies where one existed.

The second swells. Muscles that shouldn’t exist form beneath translucent skin. The right arm stretches, elongates, becomes a two-meter bone blade. The left explodes into needle-thin tentacles.

The third collapses. Loses its humanoid form entirely. Becomes a spider. Eight legs of solidified shadow. Mandibles open, revealing circles within circles of teeth.

The fourth and fifth merge. Two bodies becoming one. Larger. Stronger. Twin heads fused to the same impossible neck. Four arms ending in cleaver-sized claws.

The sixth doesn’t change shape. But weapons sprout from its body. Black bone spikes pierce its ethereal skin from the inside out. Covering it. Turning it into a two-and-a-half-meter humanoid porcupine.

The seventh… the seventh simply grows. Three meters. Four. Five. Grotesquely elongated. Arms thin as branches but long enough to scrape the ground. Fingers like roots.

All converging on Luna.

But they don’t reach her.

Because something stands between them and the girl.

Gray-and-black mist materializes.

Red Eyes.

The creature is no longer small.

It explodes in size. From stray dog to…

Beast.

As large as a bear. Larger. Canine form condensing, solidifying. Impossible muscles pulsing beneath gray-and-black mist. The mouth opens — no teeth, but something far worse: a void that pulls, that devours — and the sound that comes out…

It’s not a bark.

Not a growl.

It’s a scream.

High-pitched. Impossible. Filled with primal rage and ancient hunger.

The shadows stop. Just for a second.

But it’s enough.

Red Eyes lunges. Gray-and-black mist crashes into the first shadow — the one split into two bodies.

The impossible mouth closes around the four-armed torso.

And bites.

The shadow shrieks — wet and wrong — and dissolves. Not into normal smoke.

Into mist that Red Eyes absorbs.

Consumes.

The other six hesitate. Then panic.

Because they realize.

This creature isn’t a normal shadow. Not Illuminated.

It’s something in between.

Something that devours its own kind.

And they hate it.

### NARRATOR: NEFELI

Through my connection with Red Eyes — weak but present — I feel it.

The shadows aren’t just attacking.

It works. Just like Minus, Red Eyes — using me as a power source — can attack shadows and consume them.

They’re running.

Not toward Luna.

Toward me. Toward Red Eyes. Toward Minus, still trapped by the Illuminated’s chain.

Two leap from the ground. The spider and the elongated giant. Scaling the commercial building wall. Claws sinking into concrete and glass. Shards rain down. Alarms scream.

But more come.

From the sewer, a manhole explodes. Three shadows emerge. One reptilian — snake body with human arms sprouting every half meter. Another arachnid but wrong — ten legs, three heads, too many eyes. The third shapeless — a pulsing mass of tentacles and mouths.

From the parallel street, two more. Running on all fours like wolves. But wolves with exposed spines, ribs piercing translucent skin from the inside.

From an alley on the right, the last one. Humanoid. Almost. Arms too long. Knees bending backward. Head rotating 360 degrees as it runs.

Eleven total now.

All converging.

The spider reaches the rooftop where I am first. Mandibles open. Circles of teeth spinning.

A translucent claw reaches for me.

I don’t move.

I am penumbra. It is shadow.

The claw passes through me like smoke through smoke.

But it feels it. Feels something wrong. Something impossible.

Because I don’t exist to it.

I am invisible. Not just to the impure. But to everything. Shadows. Illuminated. Even Luna below can’t see me.

Only Minus.

Always only Minus.

The spider moves past me.

Toward its real target.

Minus.

### NARRATOR: MINUS

I’m still trapped.

The Illuminated holds my chain with one hand. Firm. Impassive.

It doesn’t pull anymore. Just holds. Making its point.

You cannot defeat me.

Eleven shadows climb buildings, burst from sewers, run through streets toward me. Fast. Hungry. Deformed nightmares.

The spider reaches the edge first. Launches itself.

Mandibles aiming for my neck.

Then the Illuminated squeezes.

The chain — my chain, my power, four years of training condensed into blue-and-black links — cracks.

Not a clean break. It fractures like glass under impossible pressure. Fissures spread through the links. Blue light leaks out. The structure unravels.

And then…

It breaks.

Simply disintegrates. As if it were nothing. As if it were insignificant.

Four years of training.

Four years of pain and sweat and blood to manifest this power.

And it breaks like nothing.

I fall.

Straight into the climbing shadows.

The spider is closest. Mandibles open.

The chain on my left arm — still intact, still functional — lashes out. Instinct. Training.

It hits the spider mid-air.

But doesn’t destroy it.

It shrieks. Twists. Slams into the wall. Three legs snap — the sound of bone breaking, even though it’s shadow.

But it doesn’t explode into smoke.

It falls. Recovers. Five legs are enough.

Shit. They’re stronger.

The elongated giant comes from the left. Arms thin as branches but fast.

The mass of tentacles from the right. Mouths opening and closing.

Simultaneous. Coordinated.

The free chain snakes out. Grabs the giant. Pulls. Smashes it into the glass building.

The surface cracks. The shadow passes through. Falls into the dark office inside.

But the tentacles slip through.

Five wrap around my ankle. Pull.

I fall sideways. Roll. The chain seeks an anchor. Hooks the rooftop edge.

I stabilize. Kick.

The tentacles release. But they leave a burn. Not physical — worse.

The reptilian one arrives. Snake body slithering. Six arms extend. Each hand holding something different.

Claw. Bone blade. Spike. Tentacle. Weapons grown from its own body.

It attacks. All six arms at once.

I dodge two. Block a third with the chain.

The other three hit.

Claw tears my shoulder.

Blade slices my thigh.

Spike pierces my side.

Pain explodes. Not physical — worse. As if the weapons touched something inside me that should never be touched.

I scream.

And the Illuminated above…

Lets go of the shattered chain remains.

Not out of mercy.

Because it’s satisfied.

Point made.

I look down.

By reflex. By instinct. Searching for escape.

And I see it.

### A CHILD · NARRATOR: MINUS

A child. Small. Alone. Exactly like Minus once was.

At the entrance of the alley. Three floors below.

Looking up with huge, frightened eyes.

And coming down the street…

Two shadow-wolves. Running on all fours. Exposed spines. Ribs piercing translucent skin.

Straight for him.

The boy doesn’t see them. Still looking up. At impossible sounds. At a battle he can’t understand.

Innocent. Unaware. Defenseless.

The wolves are ten meters away.

Five.

And I’m too far.

Three floors up. Eleven shadows attacking me. One chain broken. Body wounded.

Too far.

Unless…

No.

The free chain lashes out, searching for an anchor. But there’s no time to descend. No time to plan.

Only time to…

No. Anything but that.

The wolves are three meters from the boy.

And I have no choice.

I never did.

— “Nefeli,” I whisper. My voice breaks. Defeated. “Omega Protocol.”

Through the cord, I feel her hesitation.

— ARE YOU SURE?

I’m not. I never am.

But the boy is there. And the shadows are coming.

— “Now.”

I turn.

The nearest shadow — the shadow of a lamppost. I don’t want this, but I have no choice. I need to enter the Shadow World. Only there will I have time to save that child.

I run. Three steps. Four.

And dive.

From the lamppost’s shadow, a giant hand manifests, grabs me, and drags me inside.

Straight into the darkness of its ethereal body.

The world disappears.

### ONCE AGAIN IN THE SHADOW WORLD · NARRATOR: MINUS

Cold.

Not temperature. Something worse.

Absence. Void. The feeling of being somewhere that shouldn’t exist.

The Shadow World.

I’ve been here before. Not even ten times, mostly for training. But each time was worse than the last.

The first time was an accident. I took a long time to understand. And even now, I don’t understand what this thing is that pulls me here, to this inverted, depraved, destroyed world. I spent two minutes trapped between realities before Nefeli pulled me back. Two minutes that felt like hours.

The second was intentional. A test. How long could I stay? Five minutes before nausea. Ten before bleeding. Fifteen before almost losing consciousness.

The third…

The third time I saw things.

Things that should not be seen.

I swore never to return. But I had to a few more times. This is a resource of mine — one that must be used wisely.

And here I am.

Again.

Because a seven-year-old boy is about to die.

And I am the only one who can stop it.

But this…

This is different.

Worse.

Much worse.

The Shadow World I found four years ago in São Paulo was corrupted. Crooked buildings. Streets leading nowhere. Pulsing red sky.

But this…

Brasília.

The political heart of the country. Center of power. Where decisions affecting millions are made daily.

And its reflection here…

Is hell.

The sky isn’t just red. It’s living flesh. Pulsing. Bleeding. Black veins branching like rotten roots.

The ground isn’t concrete. It’s organic mass. Soft. Sticky. Every step sinks. Every movement tears pieces that cling to my soles.

And the smell…

God, the smell.

Decomposition. Sulfur. Something sweet and sickening like fruit rotting in the sun.

But worse than everything…

The sounds.

Screams. Everywhere and nowhere.

Not human. Not entirely.

Too high-pitched. Too sustained. Filled with despair that never ends because it can’t end because this is eternal.

Suffering condensed into sound.

And I feel it.

The weight.

Physical. Real. As if the air were solid. As if every centimeter of skin were being crushed by invisible hands.

Breathing hurts. Moving hurts. Existing hurts.

Energy drains. Fast.

Every second here costs something. Every moment steals strength I need to leave.

Fast. I need to leave fast.

I try to orient myself. But there are no references. No up or down. Left or right.

Only red-and-black chaos.

Then I see them.

Shapes at the edge of vision.

Not normal shadows. Something bigger. Older. Hungrier.

Native inhabitants of this place.

And they sense me.

Intruder.

Alive.

Broken but not dead.

— WRONG.

Claws extend from the darkness. Mouths open, revealing infinite circles of teeth.

Tentacles thick as tree trunks coil through impossible air.

Everything converges.

On me.

Wanting to tear. Rip. Consume.

— “NEFELI!”

I scream through the cord. Desperate.

— “The child’s shadow! WHERE?”

The answer comes. Weak. Distorted. But it comes.

— THERE. TWENTY METERS. LEFT AND DOWN.

Left and down make no sense here. But I trust her.

I run.

Claws pass centimeters from my face. Tentacles lash the air where I was seconds ago.

Everything wants me dead.

Everything.

The Shadow World itself rejects my presence. Like an immune system attacking a virus.

Because I shouldn’t be here. Never should have been.

I’m too alive. Too broken. Too wrong.

The organic mass beneath my feet tries to trap me. Fingers — no, claws — no, something worse — sprout from the surface, grabbing at my ankles.

I kick. Tear free. Keep moving.

There!

I see it.

Small. Clean. Innocent even in this rot.

The child’s shadow.

Like a lighthouse in a storm. Like light in absolute darkness.

I dive.

And hell lets go.

Reluctant. Furious.

But it lets go.

### BACK TO THE REAL WORLD · NARRATOR: MINUS

The real world returns.

Sound. Light. Temperature.

Clean air — so clean it hurts lungs used to rot.

I collapse to my knees. Gasping. Sweat pouring. Blood leaking from wounds that didn’t exist before.

The Shadow World always takes its price.

Always.

Matheus — the boy — steps back, frightened. But alive. Untouched.

The shadow-wolves explode into smoke when I cross back. Destroyed by contact with my body saturated with Shadow World energy.

I stand slowly.

Every muscle screams. Every nerve burns.

But I’m standing.

And I’m…

Facing away from something.

Someone.

Heat. Energy. Pulsing centimeters behind me.

Unlike anything I’ve ever felt.

Familiar yet unknown.

Powerful yet controlled.

And then I realize.

The mark on my left shoulder…

Burns.

Not like when I use the chain. Not like when shadows approach.

Different.

As if it recognizes something.

As if it responds to something.

I turn. Slowly.

And for the first time since awakening my power, I feel something even more different.

Even more powerful…
