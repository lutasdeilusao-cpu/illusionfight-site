3. THE UNIVERSE: ILLUSION FIGHT
======================================================================
In the city of Marélia, in Bravara, teenagers from all over the world
climb a global ranking by fighting in the LDI, a combat simulator. The
fight is virtual. The pain is real. Kim, a 17-year-old who sells candy on
buses and attends Elite Academy on a scholarship, fights his first LDI
match and it goes viral worldwide — not because he won, but because of
what came out of him before he did. Kim carries the Primordial Blood of
the Xakaxi, an Indigenous people who sealed away a corrupted spirit 500
years ago. That spirit, now called David Kronos, wants Kim. Whatever the
cost.

Themes: friendship, poverty and dignity, the family you choose,
Indigenous ancestry, fighting as a language.

ILLUSION TALES (short stories, free for everyone)
The characters' pasts, each told in first person.
  01 · Sonata of a Fight — Ryan's grey life at Elite Academy: his
       father, the bully Arnold, and the day he decided to end it all —
       until he bumped into the only two guys in school who have color.
  02 · Alan, the Champion — How Alan became the Crime King of Marélia,
       from the murder he witnessed at three to the day a street kid
       refused to leave his turf.
  03 · Kim and Jack's Band — Why Jack won't stop saying they have a
       band. Told by Kim, as reluctantly as humanly possible.
  04 · I Am Nina — The mother she doesn't remember, the seven years of
       poverty her father chose for her, and the talk that explained it.
  05 · Because That's How Jack Chose It — Parents who loved him and
       couldn't raise him, the streets at five, the delivery business he
       built from nothing, and a secret nobody ever asked about.
  06 · Innocent Until Proven Otherwise — Freddy's party, Jasmine, a cake
       and a knee to the face that belonged to nobody. Told by Jack, his
       way.
  07 · The Chains — Kim and Jack, covering for each other: the first
       fight against the Champion and the night a biker club took Helena
       over a debt.
illusionfight.com/historias/contos

THE SHADOW WORLD (book — Discovery Saga · Book 1, horror)
Minus is nine years old and has already lost everything three times. The
spiral burned into his shoulder draws the shadows — creatures that feed
on pain — and an Illuminated One who has hated him since the cradle.
Alone on the streets of Brasília, he learns to turn his own fear into a
weapon. Available on Amazon: https://www.amazon.com.br/dp/B0GF4N2V9G

SEA OF ASHES (book — Arc I · Escape, dark fantasy, unreleased)
In an archipelago where fuel is made from the crystallized memory of
dead gods, seventeen-year-old Elizabeth Thoss is married off to a
commander of the Admiralty. She has no proof of what he did to his
previous wives — she has something better: years of invisibility and a
memory that forgets nothing.

6. CHARACTERS
======================================================================
KIM (Joaquim Leal Reis), 17 — Xakaxi descendant. Has sold candy on buses
  since he was twelve and attends Elite Academy on a scholarship. Carries
  something ancestral he never asked for and can't control.
  "I don't beg anyone. Ever."
JACK, 17 — Green dreads, phone always in hand, laughs at everything and
  is more dangerous than he looks. Kim's best friend since they were
  twelve. Fights with the cane Vitória.
NINA — Arrived unannounced. The katana Angel, Muay Thai and a past she
  doesn't explain. Beat Kim in their first serious fight.
RYAN — Elite Academy student. Has everything, except a morning that
  doesn't hurt. Lead of the story Sonata of a Fight.
ALAN, THE CHAMPION, 24 — Became the Crime King of Marélia at eighteen.
  The rival who taught them some fights can't be won, only survived.
HELENA — Kim's adoptive mother. She was twelve when she ran away with
  him in her arms.
SHAMAN YAWANARI — Immortal Xakaxi shaman, guardian of the seal for 500
  years. Ancestral wisdom sharing space with pop culture.
DAVID KRONOS — The current name of Voa, a corrupted ancestral spirit who
  has waited centuries for the Primordial Blood.
SHUNTARO CHIRASU — World #998. Traveled nine hours from Azuma just to
  fight Kim.
LISA — Global top 500 and international pop star. Sings, dances, fights.

7. THE AUTHOR: ISAIAS LEAL
======================================================================
Isaias started working at 13, selling candy on buses in Brazil —
without knowing it, living the story he would one day write. At 17, in a
history class, he noticed nobody told the story of Brazil's Indigenous
peoples before the "discovery". That's where Illusion Fight was born: he
wrote it in Word, printed it at school and handed out the pages, and
every week his classmates demanded the next chapter.

At 30, after ten years as a civil servant, he turned his life upside
down: he studied for a scholarship, joined the games industry as an
intern and specialized in porting games to Nintendo, Xbox and
PlayStation. He has worked on international projects, including Five
Nights at Freddy's and a release in the God of War universe. Today, at
40, he is publishing the universe he has been building since he was 17 —
books, webtoon, games and soundtrack, made independently.
